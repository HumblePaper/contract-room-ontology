## Overview for deal makers

Delaware is the US state where a majority of US public companies and most venture-backed companies are incorporated, because the Delaware General Corporation Law is flexible, the state's case law is extensive, and its Court of Chancery decides corporate disputes quickly and without juries. Delaware contract law gives strong effect to what sophisticated parties have written. So caps, exclusions, survival periods and exclusive remedy provisions are generally enforced as drafted. The most important limit is that Delaware courts will not enforce a provision that claims to protect a party from liability for its own fraud in making the representations in the contract. The Court of Chancery set out that limit in its 2006 decision in ABRY Partners v F&W Acquisition.

## Key statutes

The Delaware General Corporation Law, in Title 8 of the Delaware Code, governs incorporation, charters, mergers, stockholder votes and appraisal rights. It also governs exculpation (section 102(b)(7)), under which a company's charter may remove or limit the personal liability of directors and officers to pay money damages for breaches of the duty of care. Exculpation does not reach breaches of the duty of loyalty, acts in bad faith, intentional misconduct or knowing violations of law, and for officers it does not reach claims brought by or on behalf of the company. Section 2708 of Title 6 makes a choice of Delaware law enforceable in a contract worth at least 100,000 US dollars. Section 8106(c) of Title 10 lets a written contract worth at least 100,000 US dollars set a limitation period of up to twenty years. The Delaware Revised Uniform Limited Partnership Act and the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act govern the partnerships and limited liability companies used for funds and joint ventures.

## Courts and regulators

The Delaware Court of Chancery is a court of equity that hears corporate, merger and commercial disputes without a jury. It is the forum most often chosen in merger agreements. The Delaware Superior Court hears contract claims for damages, including in its Complex Commercial Litigation Division. The Delaware Supreme Court hears appeals from both. The Delaware Division of Corporations keeps the register of Delaware entities and accepts filings of charters and certificates of merger.

## Market conventions

Public merger agreements for Delaware targets almost always choose Delaware law and the Court of Chancery. They also give the parties a right to specific performance (a court order to carry out the agreement). The 2024 merger agreement between Synopsys and Ansys follows this pattern. Venture financings on the NVCA model documents assume a Delaware company. Private acquisition agreements commonly use Delaware law even when neither party is based in the state. They rely on section 2708 of Title 6 to make that choice effective.
