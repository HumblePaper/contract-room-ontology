## Overview for deal makers

Indian contract law is written down in a statute, the Indian Contract Act 1872. The Act states many rules that English law develops through cases. Examples are the rule on remoteness of damage in section 73 and the treatment of agreed sums payable on breach in section 74. Indian courts generally enforce limitation of liability clauses agreed between commercial parties. Where bargaining power is grossly unequal, they review clauses for unconscionability and public policy under section 23 of the Contract Act. Commercial agreements governed by Indian law commonly provide for arbitration. The choice of seat (the arbitration's legal home) and the drafting of the arbitration clause matter as much as the substantive terms. Stamp duty is payable on most commercial instruments under the Indian Stamp Act 1899 and the state stamp laws. An unstamped or understamped instrument cannot be admitted in evidence until the duty and any penalty are paid.

## Key statutes

The Indian Contract Act 1872 governs formation, performance, breach and compensation. The Specific Relief Act 1963, as amended in 2018, makes specific performance (a court order to perform the contract) the ordinary remedy rather than a discretionary one. The Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 governs arbitration seated in India and international arbitration. The Companies Act 2013 governs companies, including share transfers, related-party transactions and filings with the Registrar of Companies. The Foreign Exchange Management Act 1999 and its rules control cross-border investment and payments. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 governs the processing of personal data.

## Courts and regulators

In India, commercial disputes above specified values go to the commercial courts and the commercial divisions of the High Courts under the Commercial Courts Act 2015. Final appeals go to the Supreme Court of India. The National Company Law Tribunal hears company and insolvency matters. The Securities and Exchange Board of India regulates listed companies and securities, and the Reserve Bank of India regulates banks, payments and foreign exchange. The Competition Commission of India reviews combinations (mergers and acquisitions above set thresholds), and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs keeps the company register through the Registrar of Companies.

## Market conventions

Indian commercial agreements usually cap liability at the fees paid in a stated period and exclude indirect and consequential loss. They carve out fraud, wilful misconduct and breach of confidentiality. Section 74 limits recovery of a named sum to reasonable compensation. So parties draft liquidated damages (a sum agreed in advance for a breach) as a genuine pre-estimate of loss and record the basis for it. Cross-border agreements with Indian counterparties often choose Singapore or London as the seat of arbitration. Agreements between Indian parties usually seat the arbitration in an Indian city.
