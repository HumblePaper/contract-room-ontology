## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, "Clio Completes Historic US$1 Billion vLex Acquisition, Announces US$500 Million Series G at US$5 Billion Valuation", LawSites, 10 November 2025. LawSites is an independent legal technology news site.

## What it establishes

LawSites reported that Clio completed its US$1 billion purchase of vLex, the legal research company behind the Vincent AI assistant. It reported a US$500 million Series G round and a US$350 million debt facility, which valued the combined company at US$5 billion. It says Clio was founded in 2008 as cloud practice management software for small firms. After the deal, the company said it had US$400 million in annual recurring revenue and 400,000 legal professionals as customers. Those two figures are Clio's own. The report describes Clio as based in Burnaby, British Columbia.
