## Citation

Luminance, "Contact us", the contact page at luminance.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date.

## What it establishes

Luminance lists eight offices: London, Cambridge, New York, San Francisco, Dallas, Toronto, Vancouver and Sydney. The London office is at 2 Fitzroy Place, 8 Mortimer Street. The Cambridge office is on the fifth floor of 20 Station Road. The New York office is at Suite 1503, 1230 Avenue of the Americas. The page does not say which office is the headquarters, so the entry on Luminance names none.
