## Citation

David Eberly, "DocuSign's 2026 Points to the Next Clause in AI Strategy", The National CIO Review, 18 March 2026. The figures are DocuSign's reported results, relayed by the publication.

## What it establishes

DocuSign, listed on Nasdaq as DOCU, closed its fiscal 2026 with fourth-quarter revenue of US$837 million, up 8%. Annual recurring revenue ended the year at US$3.3 billion, also up 8%. After just over 18 months on sale, Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM) reached more than US$350 million in annual recurring revenue, 10.8% of the company total. DocuSign also said its agreement repository, Navigator, held more than 200 million private consented agreements, up from 150 million in December.
