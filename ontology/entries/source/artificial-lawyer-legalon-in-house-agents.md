## Citation

Artificial Lawyer, "LegalOn Launches Family of Inhouse Agents", published 10 February 2026 under the publication's own byline. LegalOn Technologies, the subject of the report, sells contract review software.

## What it establishes

LegalOn Technologies launched five AI agents for in-house legal teams on 10 February 2026. Each agent takes one recurring job. Playbook builds a review playbook from a team's templates or existing review guidelines. Intake answers requests from the business and gathers the missing facts. Translate converts contracts and redlines between languages. Triage, released in early access, reviews high-volume, low-risk contracts and either approves them or escalates them to a lawyer. Draft, announced as coming soon, produces drafts from templates and clause libraries. LegalOn's chief executive, Daniel Lewis, described the agents as "the equivalent of additional staff". The report shows how a vendor divides in-house contract work into separate agents.
