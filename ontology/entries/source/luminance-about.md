## Citation

Luminance, "About Luminance | Contract Intelligence", the about page at luminance.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Its figures are the company's own.

## What it establishes

Luminance sells what it calls Contract Intelligence: AI that reads contracts and turns their terms into information a business can act on. The company says it was born out of the University of Cambridge. It says it has more than 1,000 customers in more than 70 countries and is trusted by more than 1,000 enterprises worldwide. The site's navigation lists its products as Negotiation, Repository and Workflows, lists Luna under its technology, and lists solutions for legal, finance, procurement, compliance, sales, executive and HR teams.
