## Citation

Amanda Robert, ABA Journal, 10 November 2025, a report that Clio completed its US$1 billion acquisition of vLex and announced a US$5 billion company valuation. The ABA Journal is the magazine of the American Bar Association.

## What it establishes

Clio completed its US$1 billion purchase of vLex on 10 November 2025. The report describes vLex as a legal intelligence and global research platform that includes Vincent AI. Clio said it would combine Vincent AI with its own software into what it calls the Intelligent Legal Work Platform. Clio also said it was now valued at US$5 billion after raising US$500 million in a Series G round led by New Enterprise Associates.
