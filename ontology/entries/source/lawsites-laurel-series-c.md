## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, LawSites, 10 June 2025, a report on the AI timekeeping company Laurel raising US$100 million in a Series C round. LawSites is a legal technology news site. The growth and customer figures come from the company.

## What it establishes

Laurel raised US$100 million in a Series C led by IVP, with GV (Google Ventures) taking part. Ryan Alshak is its founder and chief executive. The company was founded in 2016 and was originally called Time by Ping. It won the first Startup Alley at ABA Techshow in 2017. The company reported 300% growth year on year, with accounting firms making up half its business. It said its customers include more than 100 of the top legal, accounting and consulting firms in the US, UK, EU, Australia and Canada.
