## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, LawSites, 19 March 2021, a report on the investment in Relativity that put the company's valuation at a reported US$3.6 billion. LawSites is a legal technology news site.

## What it establishes

Relativity agreed a strategic growth investment from Silver Lake, the private equity firm. The deal made Silver Lake Relativity's largest shareholder, with a significant minority stake. LawSites relayed a Wall Street Journal report that the deal valued Relativity at about US$3.6 billion. The report names the other shareholders as the founder Andrew Sieja and the private equity firm Iconiq Capital, which invested US$125 million in 2015, when the company was called kCura. It describes Relativity as widely acknowledged to be the market leader for e-discovery software and as Chicago-based. Mike Gamson was chief executive at the time.
