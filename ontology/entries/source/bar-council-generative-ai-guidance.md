## Citation

The Bar Council, Information Technology Panel, "Considerations when using ChatGPT and generative artificial intelligence software based on large language models", issued 30 January 2024 and last reviewed 25 November 2025, published on barcouncilethics.co.uk. The Bar Council represents barristers in England and Wales. The document says it is not "guidance" for the purposes of the Bar Standards Board Handbook, so it has no regulatory force. It says its principles reflect current professional expectations.

## What it establishes

The Bar Council's document tells barristers in England and Wales that large language models are no substitute for their own expertise, judgment and checking. In its words, "The ultimate responsibility for all legal work remains with the barrister." It names the main risks: anthropomorphism, hallucinations (confident but false output), information disorder, bias in training data, and the exposure of confidential data. Barristers must respect legal professional privilege, confidential information and data protection when they use these tools. Its conclusion says the best-placed barristers will be "those that make the effort to understand these systems and, if appropriate, use them as tools in their practice, while maintaining control and integrity in their use." It is the England and Wales professional guidance on the first reading list.
