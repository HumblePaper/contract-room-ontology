## Citation

LDS Team, "OpenAI Partners With Ironclad on Agent Training", Lets Data Science, 6 October 2026. The article is a secondary report that relies on a Seeking Alpha story of the same date.

## What it establishes

The article reports that OpenAI is working with Ironclad, among other software companies, to pick hard, high-value tasks and turn them into research problems for training and testing AI agents. It names contract lifecycle workflows as the focus, and describes that work as including document review, clause comparison, redlining and approval routing. It says the reporting did not disclose the models, data, measures, commercial terms or timing. The entry treats it as a secondary report.
