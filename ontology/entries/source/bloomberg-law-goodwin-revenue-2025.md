## Citation

Tatyana Monnay, Bloomberg Law, 20 October 2025, a report on Goodwin Procter's record revenue from M&A and litigation work. The revenue figure comes from the firm's chairman.

## What it establishes

Goodwin's revenue reached US$2.7 billion in the year ending 30 September 2025, according to the firm's chairman, Anthony McCusker. Revenue grew more than 12% and was the firm's highest to date. Goodwin brought in more than US$1.6 million in revenue per lawyer. The firm ranked 18th in the Am Law 2024 list of firms by revenue. The report names Joshua Klatzkin as the newly elected managing partner.
