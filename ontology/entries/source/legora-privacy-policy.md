Legora, "Privacy Policy", legora.com/legal/privacy-policy, read on 15 October 2026.

## What it establishes

The policy names Legora AB, Swedish company number 559338-6872, as the company behind the service. It gives a postal address in Stockholm, Sweden. The entry relies on it for Legora's legal name and for Stockholm as its home city. The policy does not cover funding, customers or products, so those facts come from other pages.
