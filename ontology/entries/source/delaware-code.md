## Citation

Delaware Code Online, published by the Delaware Legislative Council's Division of Research at delcode.delaware.gov. A reference gives the title and the section number, so section 2708 of Title 6 is written 6 Del. C. § 2708.

## What it establishes

The Delaware Code holds the statutes that make Delaware the default US state in which to incorporate and a frequent choice of governing law. Title 8 contains the Delaware General Corporation Law, which governs mergers, stockholder approvals and appraisal rights (a dissenting stockholder's right to a court-set price). Section 2708 of Title 6 makes a choice of Delaware law enforceable in a contract worth at least 100,000 US dollars, even if the contract has no other link to Delaware. Section 8106(c) of Title 10 lets a written contract worth at least 100,000 US dollars set a limitation period of up to twenty years for claims under it.
