## Citation

iManage, "The Company", imanage.com/about/the-company, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. iManage wrote it about itself, so its figures are the company's own claims.

## What it establishes

The page says iManage is headquartered in Chicago, with development and support centres in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, India and Australia. It claims more than one million professionals as users at 4,000 organisations, including 2,500 law firms, and says 81% of AmLaw 200 firms use it. It lists its products: document and email management, security and governance, knowledge search, legal transaction management, task and workflow management, and iManage AI. It says it has more than 30 years of experience.
