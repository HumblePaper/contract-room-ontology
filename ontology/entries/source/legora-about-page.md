## Citation

Legora, "About", legora.com/about, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Legora wrote it about itself, so its figures are the company's own claims.

## What it establishes

Legora describes itself as an agentic operating system for legal work, used for research, review and drafting. It claims more than 130,000 legal professionals at more than 2,000 law firms and in-house legal teams across more than 80 markets. The page names General Catalyst among its backers. It does not state a headquarters or a founding year, so the entry takes those from other pages or leaves them out.
