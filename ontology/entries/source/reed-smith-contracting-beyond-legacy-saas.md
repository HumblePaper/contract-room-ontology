## Citation

Tyler J. Thompson and Talia Boiangin Behar, "When AI changes the deal: Contracting beyond legacy SaaS", Technology Law Dispatch, Reed Smith LLP, 28 September 2026.

## What it establishes

The article argues that standard software-as-a-service (SaaS) agreements were not written for AI that gives probable answers, changes over time and can act on its own. It recommends an AI addendum with an exhibit that lists each tool, its purpose, the data it uses and any required human review. For agents, it says, "the contract should also document permitted actions, escalation triggers and override rights." It treats training data rights as one of the most negotiated issues. It proposes service levels on accuracy, hallucination rates (confident but false output) and model drift. It also expects caps and indemnities to be revisited for AI-specific exposure. The piece sets out the contract terms a legal department needs when it buys AI tools and agents.
