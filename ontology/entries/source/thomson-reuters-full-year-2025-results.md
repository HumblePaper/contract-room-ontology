## Citation

Thomson Reuters, "Thomson Reuters Reports Fourth-Quarter and Full-Year 2025 Results", press release dated Toronto, 5 February 2026, as carried on Finviz. This is the company's own report of its results.

## What it establishes

The release gives total 2025 revenue of US$7,476 million and revenue of US$2,868 million for the Legal Professionals segment. It says organic growth in that segment came mainly from Westlaw, CoCounsel and Practical Law. It gives the share listing as TSX and Nasdaq under the symbol TRI. The release is datelined Toronto. The entry relies on it for the revenue figures, the listing and the home city.
