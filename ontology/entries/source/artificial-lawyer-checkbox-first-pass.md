## Citation

Artificial Lawyer, "Checkbox Launches 'First Pass' AI Contract Review", published 16 September 2026 under the publication's own byline. Checkbox, the subject of the report, sells legal intake and workflow software.

## What it establishes

Checkbox launched First Pass on 16 September 2026. The report describes it as an agent that reviews incoming legal requests, contracts and invoices before they reach a lawyer. For a contract, First Pass checks the incoming agreement against the team's playbook or preferred template. It then returns a risk score, a summary and a first review. For an invoice, it checks LEDES invoices (a standard electronic billing format) for arithmetic and formatting errors and against the billing guidelines. It then routes the matter to the team member with the lightest workload. The report is a clear example of an agent placed at the intake stage, in front of the lawyer.
