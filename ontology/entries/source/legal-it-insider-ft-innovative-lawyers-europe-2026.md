## Citation

Yasmin Lambert, "FT Innovative Lawyers Awards for Europe 2026: AI accelerates the pace of change", Legal IT Insider, 25 September 2026. The underlying report is the Financial Times' Innovative Lawyers Europe 2026 report at ft.com/reports/innovative-lawyers-europe. The FT site blocks automated fetching, so The Contract Room cites Legal IT Insider's account of the report.

## What it establishes

The FT's 2026 European awards found in-house legal teams changing how they work. Legal IT Insider puts it this way, in its own words: "General counsel are becoming agent builders, and in-house lawyers are rethinking what their roles mean as automation takes on more routine work." The awards drew more than 650 entries, which the FT's research partner assessed through interviews between April and July. The in-house winners named in the report included Repsol as the most innovative in-house team in Europe, and the Adecco Group for self-service tools for the business. The report is the main European evidence on this list that in-house teams are building agents themselves.
