## Citation

Sterling Miller, "Contract negotiation tactics for in-house counsel", Thomson Reuters legal blog, 19 November 2024. The byline describes Sterling Miller as "General Counsel/HILGERS GRABEN PLLC". Thomson Reuters sells legal research and practice guidance products.

## What it establishes

The article sets out ten tactics for in-house lawyers who negotiate contracts. They run from preparing and understanding leverage to taking rational positions and keeping one's word. Preparation comes first: "The time you spend preparing should dwarf the time you spend negotiating with the other side." Part of preparing is knowing the walk-away position, the point at which the company declines the deal. The fourth tactic is to create a negotiation playbook. Another, in the article's words, is that "nothing is done until everything is done". The piece is the practitioner's account of why a playbook exists before any software applies one.
