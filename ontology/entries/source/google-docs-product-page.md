## Citation

Google Workspace, "Google Docs: Online Document & PDF Editor", workspace.google.com/products/docs, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Google sells the product, so this is vendor material.

## What it establishes

The page says Google Docs lets teams draft agreements, request signatures and manage contract templates with eSignature in Docs. It describes comments for feedback inside the document, and earlier versions that can be referenced and restored at any time. It says Docs can edit Microsoft Word files directly without making a copy. Gemini, Google's AI assistant, is listed among the Workspace products.
