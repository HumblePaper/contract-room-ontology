## Citation

Zak Sos, "San Francisco-based Anthropic files for IPO with US$965 billion valuation", KTVU FOX 2, 1 June 2026. KTVU is an independent San Francisco Bay Area broadcaster.

## What it establishes

KTVU reported that Anthropic, which it describes as San Francisco-based, filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission for a proposed initial public offering. It reported that Anthropic had recently announced a US$65 billion private funding round, which put its valuation at US$965 billion. The entry relies on it for Anthropic's home city, its listing plans and that valuation.
