## Citation

Wikipedia, "Wolters Kluwer", read on 15 October 2026. Wikipedia is an encyclopedia that anyone can edit, so this entry uses it only for settled corporate facts. The company's own site refused scripted reads.

## What it establishes

Wolters Kluwer has its global headquarters in Alphen aan den Rijn, in the Netherlands. Its shares trade on Euronext Amsterdam under the symbol WKL, and it is part of the AEX index. The company took its present form in 1987, when Kluwer and Wolters Samsom merged. The article says the merger was a response to a takeover bid from Elsevier, but the 1987 news report it cites for that says the two groups announced that their merger plans pre-dated the bid, so the entry does not repeat the rationale. The article's infobox gives a founding date of 1968, the year the Wolters and Noordhoff publishing houses merged. The article lists five operating divisions as of 2026, one of which is Legal and Regulatory.
