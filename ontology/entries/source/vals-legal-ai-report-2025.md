## Citation

Vals AI, "Vals Legal AI Report", 27 February 2025, published with Legaltech Hub. Vals AI is a company that evaluates AI models. The report notes that Vals AI has a customer relationship with one or more of the participants.

## What it establishes

The report measured four legal AI tools against a group of lawyers, the lawyer baseline, on seven tasks. The tasks were data extraction, document questions and answers, summarisation, redlining, transcript analysis, chronologies and research in SEC filings. The best tool beat the lawyers on answering questions about a document, scoring 94.8% against 70.1%. Redlining went the other way. The lawyers scored 79.7% and the best tool 65.0%. The report advises that "legal AI tools should only be used for certain types of redlining tasks". Vendors chose which tasks to enter. LexisNexis withdrew from every task except legal research, so it does not appear in the report. The report's results table gives the best redlining score as 65.0%, while its notes on redlining give the best tool 59.4%. This entry uses the table figure. The report describes itself as a first-of-its-kind study and says it was developed in partnership with Legaltech Hub.
