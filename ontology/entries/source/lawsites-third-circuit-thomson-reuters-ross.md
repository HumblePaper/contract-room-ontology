## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, "3rd Circuit Rules For Thomson Reuters In Its Copyright Fight Against Legal Research Startup ROSS", LawSites, 30 September 2026. LawSites is an independent legal technology news site.

## What it establishes

LawSites reported that the US Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit ruled for Thomson Reuters on the two main issues in its case against ROSS Intelligence. The court affirmed the lower court's summary judgment, which held that Westlaw headnotes (the editors' summaries of points of law) were original enough for copyright and that ROSS's use of them to train its system was not fair use. The opinion was under seal when the report was written, and ROSS said it would appeal to the US Supreme Court. The report calls it the first federal appeals decision on fair use in AI training. It adds that the copying came before generative AI, and that ROSS built a rival research product.
