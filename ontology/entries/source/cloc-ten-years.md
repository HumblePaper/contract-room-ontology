## Citation

CLOC, "10 Years of CLOC", the history page at cloc.org, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. It is the association's own account of its history.

## What it establishes

CLOC stands for the Corporate Legal Operations Consortium. It was founded in 2015. It began when Connie Brenton, who was relocating, looked for others working in legal operations, then a new and loosely defined role. That small group grew into regular meetings and then into the association. The page lists her among its founding board members, as president from 2016 to 2019. Its first major event, in San Francisco in 2016, drew 500 attendees, and its 2017 Global Institute in Las Vegas drew 1,800.
