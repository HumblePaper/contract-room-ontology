## Citation

Microsoft Support, "Compare document differences using the legal blackline option", covering Word for Microsoft 365 and recent perpetual versions of Word. The article is published on support.microsoft.com, and Microsoft updates it as the product changes.

## What it establishes

The Microsoft article sets out the standard way to compare two versions of a contract in Word. The Compare command takes an original document and a revised one and leaves both unchanged. It then produces a third document in which every difference shows as a tracked change. A Word comparison can work at the level of whole words or single characters. It can include or leave out formatting, comments and other elements. Because the comparison is made between the two documents themselves, it measures the returned text against the text that was sent and does not depend on the counterparty's own tracked changes.
