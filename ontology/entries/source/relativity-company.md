## Citation

Relativity, "Company | About Relativity", the company page at relativity.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. It is the company's own description of itself.

## What it establishes

Relativity sells software that helps legal, corporate and government teams organise and review large volumes of data. Its main platform is Relativity aiR, which the page describes as the successor to RelativityOne, its software-as-a-service product. The page lists other products: Relativity aiR Government, which holds FedRAMP certification for US government use, Relativity claiR, a conversational AI tool for lawyers, and Gavel, a document drafting tool for Microsoft Word. It lists use cases that include e-discovery, investigations, data breach response and contract review. The headquarters is at 231 South LaSalle Street, Chicago, Illinois.
