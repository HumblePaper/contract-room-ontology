## Citation

Artificial Lawyer, "OpenAI Launches Astra For Law", published 18 September 2026 under the publication's own byline.

## What it establishes

OpenAI released Astra for Law, a configuration of its GPT-6 Astra model for legal work. It adds instructions for legal analysis and a Legal Search Index of US case law, statutes, regulations and administrative decisions. OpenAI reported that, on 200 legal research questions, the configuration passed the correctness check on 54.0% of them, against 38.7% for GPT-6 Astra using web search alone. The launch came with 26 partner plugins, from vendors including Thomson Reuters, Harvey, Legora and iManage. Access started with selected firms. The report shows the model makers moving into legal work directly, and shows the specialist vendors connecting to them through plugins. The coverage is limited to US law.
