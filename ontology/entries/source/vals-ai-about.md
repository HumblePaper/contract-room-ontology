## Citation

Vals AI, "About Vals AI", the company's about page at vals.ai, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. It is the company's own description of itself.

## What it establishes

Vals AI describes itself as an independent evaluator of artificial intelligence. It builds benchmarks and testing infrastructure that measure whether AI models can do the work of lawyers, bankers, engineers and doctors. The page says its scores come from test sets that it keeps private, so that models cannot be trained on the answers. The page does not name the company's founders, investors or location. The entry on Vals AI cites it for what the company does.
