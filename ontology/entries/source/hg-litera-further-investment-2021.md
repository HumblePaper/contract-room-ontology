## Citation

Hg, "Litera secures further investment from Hg", announcement of 5 November 2021 on hgcapital.com. Hg is the investor, so this is the investor's own word.

## What it establishes

Hg, a private equity firm, agreed to lead a further majority investment in Litera, after first investing in 2019. The terms were not disclosed. The announcement described Litera as based in Chicago, New York and London, serving more than 15,000 customers. It said Litera had made 12 acquisitions since Hg's first investment and was approaching 1,000 employees in 17 countries.
