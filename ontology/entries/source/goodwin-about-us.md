## Citation

Goodwin Procter, "About Us", the about page at goodwinlaw.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Its figures are the firm's own.

## What it establishes

Robert Eliot Goodwin and Joseph Osborne Procter Jr. started the firm in Boston in 1912. Goodwin says it now has more than 1,800 lawyers. It describes its work as litigation, regulatory advice and complex transactions for clients in the life sciences, private equity, real estate, technology and financial industries.
