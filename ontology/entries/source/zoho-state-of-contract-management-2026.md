## Citation

Zoho Contracts, "The State of Contract Management in 2026", an e-book published by Zoho in 2026. The page states no exact publication date or fieldwork period. Zoho sells contract management software.

## What it establishes

Zoho surveyed 1,151 legal professionals in ten countries: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, India, the Netherlands, the Philippines, Mexico, Australia, the United Arab Emirates and Singapore. Only 31% use a dedicated contract lifecycle management tool, and 50% still manage contracts manually. Compliance and regulatory requirements were the primary challenge for almost 43%. Contract professionals also named contract negotiation a primary concern, because of manual redlining. Budget limits (44%) and resistance to change (42%) were the main barriers to new tools. The survey is on this list for its spread across countries and for placing negotiation among the main pain points.
