## Citation

Kyle Wiggers, "DocuSign acquires AI-powered contract management firm Lexion", TechCrunch, 6 May 2024. The Wikipedia article on Docusign cites this report for the Lexion price.

## What it establishes

Docusign announced on 6 May 2024 that it was buying Lexion, a contract workflow automation startup, for US$165 million. The report says Docusign's earlier purchases included SpringCM, in July 2018 for US$220 million, and Seal Software, in February 2020 for US$188 million.
