## What it establishes

AT&T describes its legal department as collaborating "as a flagship in-house legal design partner with OpenAI". The two companies will design, build and evaluate legal workflows with attorney oversight. The announcement places the work on LegalEdge, the team inside AT&T's legal department that started in January 2026 and handled nearly 100 matters in its first six months. It is the primary source for the design-partner description. The Contract Room relies on the trade reports for everything else in the announcement.
