## Citation

Electronic Data Gathering, Analysis, and Retrieval system (EDGAR), run by the US Securities and Exchange Commission and free to search at sec.gov. Every filing on EDGAR carries an accession number and a filing date. Together they give a stable reference for citation.

## What it establishes

EDGAR is the main public record of what US public companies have agreed in their material deals. A public company that signs a material definitive agreement reports it on Form 8-K within four business days. The company usually files the agreement itself as an exhibit. So the full text of a public merger agreement, credit agreement or significant commercial contract can be read on EDGAR. The Contract Room cites EDGAR filings as the basis for its deal entries, and for claims about the positions that real agreements take on real clauses.
