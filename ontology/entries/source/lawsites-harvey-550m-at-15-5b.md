## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, "Harvey Raises Another US$550M At A US$15.5B Valuation", LawSites, 9 September 2026. LawSites is an independent legal technology news site.

## What it establishes

LawSites reported that Harvey raised US$550 million at a US$15.5 billion valuation, in a round co-led by Diffusion and Lightspeed. By the author's count, Harvey had raised more than US$1.5 billion since it started in 2022. The report cites co-founder Winston Weinberg for more than US$400 million in annual recurring revenue and more than 3,000 customers, including 80% of the top 100 law firms. Those revenue and customer figures are the company's own. The report says Harvey plans more specialised products for contracting, litigation, deals and compliance.
