## Citation

Litera, "About Us", the company's story page at litera.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Its figures are the company's own.

## What it establishes

Litera says it serves more than 15,000 customers worldwide and more than 1 million daily users, including 99% of the Am Law 100 (the hundred largest US law firms by revenue). It says it has served the legal industry for more than 30 years. The page lists businesses it has brought in, among them Kira and Transact for the practice of law, and Foundation and Clocktimizer for the business of law. It gives the address as 227 West Monroe Street, Chicago, Illinois. It names Lito as its legal AI agent and Litera Draft as one of its products, and says its products work inside Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. It names Avaneesh Marwaha as chief executive, appointed in October 2024, and says he was previously chief executive from 2016 to 2022.
