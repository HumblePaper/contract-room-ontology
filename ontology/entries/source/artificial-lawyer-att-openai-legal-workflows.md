## Citation

Artificial Lawyer, "AT&T + OpenAI Partner for Legal AI Workflows", published 8 October 2026 under the publication's own byline. Artificial Lawyer is a legal technology news site based in London. AT&T's own announcement is a separate source entry.

## What it establishes

AT&T LegalEdge is partnering with OpenAI to develop AI-enabled legal workflows. LegalEdge is a team inside AT&T's legal department that started in January 2026. It handled nearly 100 matters in its first six months. Its staff are attorneys, paralegals, technologists, legal operations specialists and e-discovery experts. The report quotes David McAtee, AT&T's general counsel: "It gives us an opportunity to rethink how legal work moves through an organization". The report says that legal judgment stays with people. AT&T's own announcement, not this report, describes AT&T as OpenAI's design partner.
