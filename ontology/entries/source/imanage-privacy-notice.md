## Citation

iManage, "Privacy Notice", imanage.com/about/privacy-notice, read on 15 October 2026.

## What it establishes

The notice names iManage LLC as the company behind the service, at 71 South Wacker Drive, Chicago, Illinois. It names iManage EMEA Limited at 1 Phipp Street, London, for its European business. The entry relies on it for iManage's legal name and its Chicago address. The notice says nothing about ownership or customers, so those facts come from other pages.
