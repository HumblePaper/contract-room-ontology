## Citation

vLex, "About vLex (part of Clio)", the company's about page at vlex.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. It is the company's own account, now published under Clio.

## What it establishes

Lluis and Angel Faus founded vLex in Barcelona in 2000 to make legal knowledge easier to reach through technology. vLex combined with Fastcase in 2023, creating a large legal database of content, authority data, dockets and analytics. vLex built Vincent, its AI tool for legal research and analysis. The page presents vLex as part of Clio, the law practice software company founded in 2008 by Jack Newton and Rian Gauvreau. The site's products are Vincent, the vLex Library, Docket Alarm, vLex Labs and the Fastcase Library.
