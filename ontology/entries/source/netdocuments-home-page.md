NetDocuments, "Document and Email Management for Legal Firms and Departments", netdocuments.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. NetDocuments wrote it about itself, so its figures are the company's own claims.

## What it establishes

The page describes NetDocuments as a cloud document management platform for law firms, in-house legal departments and public bodies. It lists email filing by client and matter, Microsoft Teams integration, eSignature, a legal AI assistant and tabular review. It claims more than 7,000 customers worldwide. Its footer gives the address 2500 Executive Parkway, Suite 300, Lehi, Utah.
