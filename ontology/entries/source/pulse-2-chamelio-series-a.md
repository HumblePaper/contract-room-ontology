## Citation

Amit Chowdhry, a report of Chamelio's US$26m Series A led by Entrée Capital, Pulse 2.0, 23 September 2026. Artificial Lawyer reported the same round on 22 September 2026.

## What it establishes

Chamelio, a New York company founded in 2024 that sells AI software to corporate legal departments, raised a US$26 million Series A (an early round of venture funding) led by Entrée Capital. The report states that Chamelio "aims to offer businesses an alternative to legacy contract lifecycle management software" by combining AI agents with human oversight. Contract lifecycle management (CLM) software stores contracts and routes them for approval. Chamelio said it would spend part of the money on moving customers off legacy contract management systems. The round shows investors paying for agents sold as an alternative to CLM software.
