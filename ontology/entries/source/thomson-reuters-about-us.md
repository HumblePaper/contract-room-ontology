## What it establishes

Thomson Reuters describes itself as an AI and technology company that sells content and workflow tools to professionals. It serves legal, tax and accounting, risk and fraud, trade and news customers. The page lists its legal products: Westlaw for legal research, Practical Law for guides, templates and checklists written by more than 650 attorney-editors, HighQ for collaboration and workflow, and CoCounsel Legal, its AI assistant. The entry relies on it for that product list.
