## Citation

Lizette Chapman, "Keith Rabois Bets on Legal AI Technology With Startup Spellbook", Bloomberg News on Bloomberg Law, 9 October 2025. The opening paragraphs are free to read and the rest is behind a paywall.

## What it establishes

Bloomberg reported that Spellbook raised US$50 million in a round led by Keith Rabois of Khosla Ventures. It valued Spellbook at US$350 million and described it as a Toronto-based start-up that helps speed the writing of contracts. Threshold Ventures and the existing investor Inovia Capital also took part. The entry relies on the free opening paragraphs only.
