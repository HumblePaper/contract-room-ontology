## Citation

Echo Wang and Jody Godoy, "Exclusive: Anthropic says rogue AI agents pose uncertain legal risk for the company", Reuters, 29 September 2026, as carried by The Star (Malaysia) on 30 September 2026.

## What it establishes

Reuters reported that Anthropic's prospectus for its planned stock market debut says it could face claims over the actions of autonomous AI agents, and that the law is unsettled. The prospectus says the limits on Anthropic's liability in its contracts may not be "enforceable or adequate" against such claims. The report gives the questions open in law, including whether an agent can bind the user who deployed it. The entry relies on it for that contracting risk.
