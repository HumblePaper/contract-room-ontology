## Citation

Harvey, "Privacy Policy", harvey.ai/legal/privacy-policy, read on 15 October 2026.

## What it establishes

The policy names Harvey AI Corporation as the US company behind the service, at 201 3rd Street, Suite 500, San Francisco, California. It also names an Irish company, Harvey AI Ireland Limited, in Dublin, for users in the European Union. The entry relies on it for Harvey's legal name and the city of its US office. The policy says nothing about funding or customers.
