## Citation

Amit Chowdhry, Pulse 2.0, 15 August 2026, a report on Vals AI's US$40 million Series A at a US$400 million valuation, with revenue up eightfold. Pulse 2.0 is a technology news site. Its growth figures come from the company.

## What it establishes

Vals AI raised a US$40 million Series A at a US$400 million valuation. Andreessen Horowitz led the round. The existing investors 8VC and Bloomberg Beta took part, and HRT Ventures and Next Ladder Ventures joined as new investors. The report gives the company's own growth figures: revenue eight times all of 2025, a customer base twice the size and a team three times larger over six months. It also reports that model cards from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and xAI have cited Vals AI results.
