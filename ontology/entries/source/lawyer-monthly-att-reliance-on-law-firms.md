## Citation

Matt Johnson, "AT&T Uses AI to Reduce Reliance on Outside Law Firms", Lawyer Monthly, 9 October 2026.

## What it establishes

AT&T set up LegalEdge in January 2026 to handle legal work inside the company with the help of AI. The headline states the aim, which is to rely less on outside law firms. The report says the OpenAI collaboration will focus on routine legal tasks, knowledge management and systems that support lawyers in giving advice. It names document review, legal research and drafting as work that has historically produced large billable hours for outside firms. It also states that lawyers keep control of legal decisions. In the magazine's own paraphrase, David McAtee, AT&T's general counsel, described the technology as an opportunity to reconsider how legal work is allocated and how lawyers use their time.
