## Citation

Ironclad, "About us", ironcladapp.com/about-us, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Ironclad wrote it about itself, so its figures are the company's own claims.

## What it establishes

The page says the lawyer Jason Boehmig and the engineer Cai Wangwilt founded Ironclad in 2014. It says Ironclad came out of Y Combinator and opened its first headquarters in San Francisco in 2015. Its timeline records a Series A led by Accel in 2017, a later Series E round and the hire of Dan Springer, a former Docusign chief executive, as chief executive in 2025. It claims more than two billion contracts processed on its platform.
