## Citation

Ironclad, "Ironclad Launches New AI Agents Grounded in Commercial Context, Helping Teams Negotiate Better", press release, 8 October 2026. Ironclad sells the products it describes, so this is vendor material.

## What it establishes

Ironclad announced Ironclad Agent, a Contract Knowledge Graph, policy-based access control and a Workflow Designer Agent. The release says the agent lets legal, procurement and sales teams search and act on their contracts in plain language, and that the graph links clauses, obligations and past decisions across a company's contracts. It quotes chief executive Dan Springer on making that history usable when a team needs it. The boilerplate describes Ironclad as an AI contracting platform that covers the whole contract lifecycle. The release is datelined San Francisco.
