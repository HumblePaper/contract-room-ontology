## Citation

Hg, "Litera", portfolio page on hgcapital.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Hg is the investor, so this is the investor's own word.

## What it establishes

Hg lists Litera among its current portfolio companies, with a first investment in 2019 and a further investment in 2021. The page describes Litera as a legal AI platform for the practice and business of law, with more than 15,000 customers and more than 1 million daily users, including 99% of the Am Law 100 (the hundred largest US law firms by revenue). The entry on Litera cites it for Hg's continuing ownership.
