## Citation

Docusign, "Docusign and Salesforce Launch Intelligent Agreement Management Natively Across Salesforce to Power Agentic Agreement Workflows", PR Newswire release datelined San Francisco, 13 October 2026, as carried on Finviz. Docusign sells the platform, so this is vendor material.

## What it establishes

Docusign announced that its Intelligent Agreement Management platform now runs natively in Salesforce, Agentforce and Slack, extending a partnership of more than 20 years. The release says a team can request a contract, pull live customer and pricing data, apply approved terms and route it to legal in one Slack thread. Legal and contract teams can review contracts and track renewals inside Salesforce. It names Salesforce as NYSE: CRM and says Docusign was named a 2027 Salesforce Partner of the Year.
