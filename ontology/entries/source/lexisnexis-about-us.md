## Citation

LexisNexis, "About LexisNexis", the about page at lexisnexis.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Its figures are the company's own.

## What it establishes

LexisNexis describes itself as a data and analytics company with 11,900 colleagues serving customers in more than 150 countries. It is a division of RELX, alongside Elsevier, LexisNexis Risk Solutions and Reed Exhibitions. The page says its legal and news database holds more than 200 billion documents and records, with 4 million new documents added each day.
