## Citation

Laurel, "AI Timekeeping & Work Intelligence Platform", the home page at laurel.ai, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Its claims are the company's own.

## What it establishes

Laurel sells software that records time automatically from the tools professionals already use. It sells to legal, accounting and consulting firms. For lawyers, it records billable time and drafts time entry narratives that meet billing rules. The page lists checks against billing rules at the point of time entry, and a billing product that reviews bills and flags work outside the agreed scope, which the site menu marks as coming soon. It names two further products: Laurel Signal, for project profitability, and Laurel Work Studio, for dividing work between people and AI. The company claims its users recover up to 30 minutes a day.
