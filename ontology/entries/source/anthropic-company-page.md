## Citation

Anthropic, "Company", anthropic.com/company, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date.

## What it establishes

Anthropic describes itself as an AI safety and research company. It says it is a public benefit corporation (a company that must pursue a stated purpose alongside profit), with the purpose of developing advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity. Its board is elected by stockholders and by a Long-Term Benefit Trust. The page names Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei among the directors. Its product list includes Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork and Claude for Microsoft 365.
