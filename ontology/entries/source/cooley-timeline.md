## Citation

Cooley, "Timeline", the firm history page at cooley.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. It is the firm's own account.

## What it establishes

Cooley was founded in the 1920s in the Humboldt Bank Building on Market Street in San Francisco, and marked its centennial in 2020. It says it advised on the formation of Raychem and National Semiconductor in the 1950s and opened its first Silicon Valley office in 1980. The firm says it took Genentech and Amgen public in the 1980s and Qualcomm in 1992.
