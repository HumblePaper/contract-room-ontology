## Citation

NetDocuments, "NetDocuments to Accelerate Growth with Strategic Investment from Warburg Pincus", press release, 25 May 2021. This is the company's own announcement.

## What it establishes

NetDocuments announced a definitive agreement for a significant investment by Warburg Pincus. Warburg Pincus bought the interest held by Clearlake Capital Group. Cove Hill Partners, an existing investor, stayed on. The boilerplate says NetDocuments was founded in 1999 and is based in Lehi, Utah. It did not give a price.
