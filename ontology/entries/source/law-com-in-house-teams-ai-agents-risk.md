## Citation

Trudy Knockless, "How In-House Teams Are Using AI Agents, Without Letting Risk Run Wild", Legaltech News on Law.com, 19 May 2026. The full article needs a free Law.com account.

## What it establishes

In-house legal teams are giving AI agents high-volume, multi-step work while lawyers keep judgment, privilege and risk oversight. The article defines an agent as software that takes a goal and carries out a series of steps to reach it, often across several systems. It separates task-focused agents from more autonomous "agentic AI". The control it reports is plain: limit what the agent may do. Michele Lee, general counsel of Super.com, is quoted: "It's about being thoughtful about the instructions, access and permissions that you give to the agent." The article is the practitioner's case for scoped permissions as the first control on any agent.
