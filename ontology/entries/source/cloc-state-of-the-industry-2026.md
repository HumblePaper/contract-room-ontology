## Citation

CLOC, "CLOC Releases 2026 State of the Industry Report: Rising Legal Demand Outpaces Budget and Staffing Growth, Forcing Operational Shift", 2 March 2026.

## What it establishes

The 2026 report draws on the 2025 Harbor Law Department Survey, run with CLOC. It covered leaders of 135 law departments in more than 15 industries. The full report is open only to CLOC members. Demand is rising fastest in regulatory compliance, named by 63% as a driver of workload, and in cybersecurity, named by 58%. Resources are not keeping pace. Only 32% expect attorney headcount to grow. 47% expect inside legal spend to rise. 37% expect outside counsel spend to rise, down from 58% the year before. The report also finds that 85% of departments now have dedicated AI oversight or resources.
