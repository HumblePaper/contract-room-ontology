## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, "LexisNexis Unveils the 'Next Generation' of Its Protégé General AI, Callling It the 'Most Integrated Legal AI Workflow Solution'", LawSites, 10 December 2025. The misspelling "Callling" is in the original headline. LawSites is a legal technology news site.

## What it establishes

LexisNexis released a new version of Protégé General AI within its Lexis+ AI workflow. Users choose between two modes. Protégé Legal AI is tuned for legal drafting and analysis. Protégé General AI runs general-purpose models from OpenAI and Anthropic, among others, grounded in web content and LexisNexis content. The product combines three kinds of material: documents the customer uploads, open web search results and the LexisNexis legal database. The report says Protégé General AI had a preview release in August 2025 and a commercial release in October 2025, and names the business as LexisNexis Legal & Professional.
