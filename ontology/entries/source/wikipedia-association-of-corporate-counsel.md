## Citation

Wikipedia, "Association of Corporate Counsel", read on 15 October 2026. Wikipedia is an encyclopedia that anyone can edit, and this article carries a notice that it needs more citations. This entry uses it only for a settled fact. The association's own site, acc.com, sits behind a bot wall that refuses scripted reads.

## What it establishes

The Association of Corporate Counsel (ACC) has its headquarters in Washington, D.C. The article says the association was founded as the American Corporate Counsel Association (ACCA) in 1983. Canadian Lawyer instead dates its founding to 11 March 1982, so The Contract Room does not take the founding year from this article. The article's membership figures carry no citation, so they are not used.
