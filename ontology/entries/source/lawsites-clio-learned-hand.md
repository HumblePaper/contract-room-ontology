## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, "Clio Acquires Learned Hand, Giving Its New Judiciary Business An Existing Product", LawSites, 30 September 2026. LawSites is an independent legal technology news site.

## What it establishes

LawSites reported that Clio bought Learned Hand, a start-up that builds AI only for judges and courts. Clio did not disclose the terms. The report says the purchase gives Clio's new judiciary business a product already in use. It also says the vLex purchase gave Clio a collection of more than one billion legal documents. The entry relies on it for Clio's most recent purchase.
