## Citation

Fatimah Misbah Hussain, TECHi, 19 February 2025, a report on Luminance's US$75 million Series C. TECHi is a technology news site.

## What it establishes

Luminance raised a US$75 million Series C led by Point72 Private Investments. Forestay Capital, RPS Ventures and Schroders Capital joined, with existing investors including March Capital, National Grid Partners and the law firm Slaughter and May. The round brought Luminance's total raised to US$165 million, with more than US$115 million in the previous twelve months. The report names Eleanor Lightbody as chief executive, who took over from the founders after the Series A. It says Luminance then had more than 700 clients in more than 70 countries, including AMD, Hitachi, LG Chem, SiriusXM, Rolls-Royce and Lamborghini.
