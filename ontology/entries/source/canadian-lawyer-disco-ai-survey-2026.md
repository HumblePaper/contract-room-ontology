## Citation

Bernise Carolino, "Clients pressure firm and in-house lawyers to adopt legal AI: DISCO survey", Canadian Lawyer, 8 October 2026. DISCO sells legal AI software. Ari Kaplan Advisors ran the survey for DISCO in the summer of 2026. The article does not say which countries the respondents work in.

## What it establishes

The survey covered 104 people at law firms and corporate legal departments, with 33 longer interviews. Among in-house respondents, 47% felt pressure to adopt legal AI from their company's board or senior executives, up from 18% a year before. Among law firm respondents, 64% felt pressure from clients to adopt legal AI, up from 39%. 72% described themselves as somewhat or very confident in legal AI for document review compared with manual review, up from 53%. DISCO's James Park said that "pricing transparency has become a bigger constraint on adoption than output". The article also reports that legal AI pricing has moved from annual subscriptions to charges based on use. The survey is small, and it comes from a vendor.
