## Citation

Harvey Team, "Contract Review Agents That Turn Past Deals Into Better Outcomes", Harvey blog, 10 September 2026. Harvey sells the product the post describes, so this is vendor marketing.

## What it establishes

Harvey describes review agents that a legal team builds from its precedents, guidelines and playbooks. Each agent checks a counterparty's redlines against the team's standards and against past deals. The post states a limit of playbooks that matters for any review agent: "They can capture a team's stated positions, but not always the nuance behind them." Its examples of that nuance are acceptable fallback ranges and the patterns that show what a team will actually accept. The post is on this list for that account of the gap between a written playbook and real negotiating practice. Its product claims are the vendor's own.
