## Citation

Synopsys, Inc., Current Report on Form 8-K reporting an event of 15 January 2024, filed on EDGAR under accession number 0001193125-24-008120. Exhibit 2.1 is the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated 15 January 2024 among Synopsys, Inc., ALTA Acquisition Corp. and ANSYS, Inc. Exhibit 99.1 is the joint press release of 16 January 2024.

## What it establishes

The Synopsys 8-K sets out the parties to the merger and the price: 197.00 US dollars in cash and 0.3450 of a share of Synopsys common stock for each Ansys share. It records two termination fees, payable in specified circumstances: 950 million US dollars by Ansys and 1.5 billion US dollars by Synopsys. It also records a bridge financing commitment of 16 billion US dollars, and an outside date (the deadline to complete) of 15 January 2025, which could be extended to 15 January 2026. The merger agreement filed as Exhibit 2.1 states that Delaware law governs it and that the Delaware Court of Chancery is the chosen forum. The joint press release of 16 January 2024 names the financial and legal advisers to each party.
