## Citation

Mike Scarcella, "Google expands Gemini Enterprise AI platform for law firms, lawyers", Reuters, 25 August 2026, as carried by The Star (Malaysia).

## What it establishes

Reuters reported that Alphabet's Google added tools for lawyers and law firms to its Gemini Enterprise platform, under the name Gemini Enterprise for Legal. Google said the platform lets firms choose among AI models and connects to Thomson Reuters, Harvey, Legora and other legal systems. It named Weil Gotshal, Cleary Gottlieb, Freshfields and Williams & Connolly among the firms working with it. The report also notes that Thomson Reuters had launched its own model, Thomson 1.0, a day earlier.
