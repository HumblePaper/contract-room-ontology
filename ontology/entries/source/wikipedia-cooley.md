## Citation

Wikipedia, "Cooley LLP", read on 15 October 2026. Wikipedia is an encyclopedia that anyone can edit, so this entry uses it only for settled facts about the firm. Cooley's own about page does not name a headquarters.

## What it establishes

Cooley has its headquarters in Palo Alto, California. It was founded in San Francisco in 1920. The article names Arthur Cooley and Louis Crowley as founders and describes the firm's technology and life sciences work, but the entry on Cooley does not rely on it for those points.
