## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, "Private Equity Firm Acquires NetDocuments", LawSites, 25 May 2021. LawSites is an independent legal technology news site. It relies in part on a Legal IT Insider report.

## What it establishes

LawSites reported that the private equity firm Warburg Pincus bought NetDocuments from Clearlake Capital Group, and that Cove Hill kept its stake. Citing Legal IT Insider, it reported an unnamed source's figure of US$1.4 billion for the deal. It added that chief executive Josh Baxter declined to confirm that figure. The entry treats the value as unconfirmed.
