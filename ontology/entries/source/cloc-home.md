## Citation

CLOC, "The Leading Peer-Driven Community for Legal Operations", the home page at cloc.org, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Its figures are the association's own and appear as images on the page.

## What it establishes

CLOC describes itself as the leading peer-driven, not-for-profit community for legal operations. It brings together legal operations professionals, service providers and others to push for innovation and common standards in the field. The page gives its membership as more than 6,300 members in more than 60 countries. It lists its events, including the CLOC Global Institute, the CLOC Europe Summit and the CLOC Australia Summit, and a framework for the field called the CLOC Core 12.
