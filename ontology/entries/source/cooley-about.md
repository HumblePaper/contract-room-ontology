## Citation

Cooley, "About Cooley", the about page at cooley.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Its figures are the firm's own.

## What it establishes

Cooley says it has nearly 1,400 lawyers in 19 offices across the United States, Asia and Europe. It has more than 400 partners in a single-tier partnership (one class of partner, all sharing in profits) and a total workforce of more than 3,000. It names Rachel Proffitt as chief executive. Its practices are corporate, litigation, regulatory and intellectual property. The firm says it has handled more than 1,600 M&A transactions worth more than US$750 billion since 2021, and calls itself the leading firm for technology and life sciences IPOs.
