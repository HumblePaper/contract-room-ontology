## Citation

OpenAI, "Privacy policy", openai.com/policies/row-privacy-policy, updated 6 February 2026, read in a browser on 15 October 2026. The page refuses scripted fetches.

## What it establishes

The policy names OpenAI OpCo, LLC, with its registered office at 1455 Third Street, San Francisco, California, as the controller for users outside Europe. It names OpenAI Ireland Limited in Dublin for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland. The entry relies on it for OpenAI's home city only. The policy says nothing about legal products.
