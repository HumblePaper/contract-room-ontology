## Citation

Microsoft, "Facts About Microsoft", news.microsoft.com, read on 15 October 2026. The headcount table is stated as at 30 September 2026.

## What it establishes

The fact sheet gives Microsoft Corporation's address as One Microsoft Way, Redmond, Washington. Its timeline says Microsoft was founded in 1975, its stock went public on 13 March 1986, and Satya Nadella became chief executive on 4 February 2014. It gives a worldwide headcount of 223,000 at 30 September 2026. The entry relies on it for those company facts.
