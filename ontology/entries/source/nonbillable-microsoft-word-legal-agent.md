## Citation

Oliver Attinger, "Microsoft shakes up legal tech with contract review agent", Non-Billable, 30 April 2026. Non-Billable is an independent UK legal news site.

## What it establishes

Non-Billable reported that Microsoft launched a contract review Legal Agent inside Word. The agent reviews contracts clause by clause against a team's playbook (its written negotiating positions), compares versions, flags risks and proposes edits, keeping formatting and tracked changes within Word. The report links the launch to Microsoft's hire earlier in 2026 of an 18-person team from the legal AI start-up Robin. It says the agent overlaps with tools sold by legal AI vendors that work in Word.
