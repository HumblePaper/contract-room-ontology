## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, "Anthropic Goes All-In on Legal, Releasing More Than 20 Connectors and 12 Practice-Area Plugins for Claude", LawSites, 12 May 2026. LawSites is an independent legal technology news site.

## What it establishes

LawSites reported that Anthropic released more than 20 connectors linking Claude to legal software and 12 plugins for practice areas, including commercial and corporate work. Contract and document connectors included Ironclad, DocuSign, iManage and NetDocuments. The release built on a legal plugin for Claude Cowork, Anthropic's desktop agent, released in February 2026. It says Claude for Word started in beta in April with contract review as its first example. Legal technology shares fell sharply after the February plugin.
