## Citation

Salesforce, "We're Salesforce, the #1 AI CRM.", salesforce.com/company/about-us, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Salesforce wrote it about itself, so its claims are the company's own.

## What it establishes

Salesforce says it opened in 1999 and brought customer relationship management software (the systems sales and service teams use to track customers) to the cloud. It now sells Agentforce, its platform for AI agents, alongside Slack, Tableau and its sales, service and commerce products. The entry relies on it for Salesforce's founding year and product lines.
