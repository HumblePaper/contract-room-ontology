## Citation

Bob Ambrogi, "Yesterday Came OpenAI's Launch Into Legal, Now Come the Litany of Vendor Integrations", LawSites, 18 September 2026. LawSites is an independent legal technology news site.

## What it establishes

LawSites reported that OpenAI's Astra for Law launch came with 26 ChatGPT plugins from legal technology vendors, of which OpenAI named only Harvey, iManage, Legora and Thomson Reuters. OpenAI did not answer a request for the full list. The report then lists vendors that announced plugins. Among them, the Ironclad plugin lets ChatGPT search an Ironclad contract repository in plain language. NetDocuments and Clio also announced integrations.
