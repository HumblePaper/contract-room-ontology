## Citation

SiliconANGLE, "Digital contract management startup Ironclad raises US$150M on US$3.2B valuation", 18 January 2022. SiliconANGLE is an independent technology news site.

## What it establishes

SiliconANGLE reported that Ironclad raised US$150 million in a Series E round at a US$3.2 billion valuation, led by Franklin Templeton. BOND, YC Continuity, Emergence, Lux, Haystack, Accel and Sequoia Capital also took part. The report put Ironclad's total funding at US$333 million. It names Jason Boehmig as co-founder and chief executive at the time, and lists customers including Dropbox, Mastercard and L'Oreal.
