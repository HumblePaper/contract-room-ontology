## Citation

Conga, "AI in Contracts Trend Report: 2026 Research Insights", 29 May 2026, sponsored by Salesforce. Conga sells contract lifecycle management software, meaning software that stores contracts and routes them for approval. The full report needs registration.

## What it establishes

Conga surveyed 250 senior contract professionals in early 2026, across legal, procurement, sales operations and compliance. The respondents worked in healthcare, financial services, technology and manufacturing. 95% said their organisation uses AI in contract lifecycle management. Only 24% considered their contract management optimised. 92% still require a person to review AI output. The report finds that most AI use is reactive, such as search, reporting and risk assessment. It names governance and trust as the main obstacles to wider use. It is on this list because it shows that human review of AI output is standard practice.
