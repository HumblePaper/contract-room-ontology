## Citation

Clio, "About Clio / Themis Solutions Inc.", clio.com/about, read in a browser on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date, and it refuses scripted fetches. Clio wrote it about itself, so its figures are the company's own claims.

## What it establishes

The page's title gives the company's name as Themis Solutions Inc. The page says it is headquartered in Vancouver, Canada. It says Jack Newton and Rian Gauvreau co-founded Clio in 2008. It claims more than 2,000 employees, offices in cities including Toronto, London, Dublin and Sydney, and more than 400,000 legal professionals as users in more than 130 countries. It calls its product the Intelligent Legal Work Platform.
