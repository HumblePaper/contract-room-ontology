## Citation

Legora, "Salesforce selects Legora to further support its global legal team operations", press release, 16 September 2026. Legora sells the product the release describes, so this is vendor material.

## What it establishes

Legora announced that Salesforce adopted Legora across its Legal and Corporate Affairs organisation in North America, EMEA and APAC. The release says Legora sits alongside Salesforce's own Agentforce and Slack in that team. It quotes Erica Swainson, Salesforce's vice president for legal innovation and strategic initiatives, on using the tools for research and drafting. Its boilerplate then claimed more than 100,000 legal professionals at more than 1,800 firms and in-house teams across more than 50 markets. Max Junestrand is named as chief executive and co-founder.
