## Citation

Wikipedia, "LexisNexis", read on 15 October 2026. Wikipedia is an encyclopedia that anyone can edit, so this entry uses it only for settled corporate facts.

## What it establishes

LexisNexis has its headquarters in the Helmsley Building in New York City. It dates from 1970, and its parent company is RELX, formerly Reed Elsevier. The article traces part of the business back to Butterworths, the legal publisher founded by Henry Butterworth in 1818. The article also says LexisNexis launched Protégé in 2025, but the entry does not rely on that sentence.
