## What it establishes

Google says Larry Page and Sergey Brin met at Stanford University in 1995 and built a search engine first called Backrub. It says Google Inc. was born in August 1998, after a US$100,000 cheque from Andy Bechtolsheim. It says the company later moved to its current headquarters, the Googleplex, in Mountain View, California. The entry relies on it for Google's founders, founding year and home city.
