## What it establishes

The Association of Corporate Counsel (ACC) ran the 2023 survey with Exterro to understand the role of the chief legal officer in modern business. ACC says almost 900 general counsel and chief legal officers from 35 countries, in 20 industry sectors, took part. The survey covers four topics: the role and reach of the chief legal officer, the value of the legal department to the company, the political and regulatory landscape, and the outlook for the legal department in the coming year. ACC says the results show the chief legal officer's growing influence in company leadership and wider oversight of other company functions. The full report is open only to ACC members. The Contract Room never repeats a figure from the survey without the edition and year that the figure comes from.
