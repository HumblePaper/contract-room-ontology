## Citation

"Contract Negotiation and Drafting: A Practical Guide for In-House Counsel", ACC Jobline career resources, 2026. ACC Jobline is the job board and career site of the Association of Corporate Counsel. The page refuses most scripted reads. The Contract Room and its second checker confirmed its text through search engine copies of the page.

## What it establishes

The article treats contract negotiation and drafting as a skill of high value for in-house lawyers in 2026. It notes that an in-house team, unlike a law firm, lives with the counterparty after signing and absorbs the operational consequences of every clause. It describes a playbook as "a documented set of preferred positions, acceptable fallbacks, and hard limits for each recurring clause". That lets a department of a few lawyers handle a large contract volume consistently. It is a career-site article, not formal guidance from the association.
