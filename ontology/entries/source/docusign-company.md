## Citation

Docusign, "About Docusign", the company page at docusign.com, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Its figures are the company's own.

## What it establishes

Docusign says it changed how the world agrees with eSignature and now sells a category it calls Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM). The page gives the company's figures: more than 1.9 million paying customers, more than a billion users and customers in more than 180 countries. The page's product menu describes IAM as a way to create, commit to and manage agreements. Its products include eSignature, Intelligent Agreement Management, contract lifecycle management (software that tracks a contract from request to renewal), Iris, which analyses agreement data, and electronic notarisation.
