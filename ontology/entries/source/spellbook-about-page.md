## Citation

Spellbook, "About Spellbook - Legal AI Software for Lawyers", spellbook.com/about, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Spellbook wrote it about itself, so its claims are the company's own.

## What it establishes

The page says Spellbook makes AI software for lawyers and works directly in Microsoft Word. It says the product is tuned for contracting and runs on OpenAI's GPT-5 and other large language models. It claims customers in more than 80 countries and more than 5,000 legal teams. Its product menu lists Review, Draft, Compare, Ask, Playbooks, Associate and ACM, its autonomous contract management product. It does not state a headquarters.
