## Citation

American Bar Association Standing Committee on Ethics and Professional Responsibility, Formal Opinion 512, "Generative Artificial Intelligence Tools", 29 July 2024. The PDF on americanbar.org refuses scripted fetches, so The Contract Room could not read it directly. Everything this entry says about the opinion is cited through The Bar Examiner's analysis, which is a separate source entry.

## What it establishes

According to The Bar Examiner's analysis, the opinion applies the ABA's Model Rules of Professional Conduct to generative AI. A lawyer who uses an AI tool keeps the duties of competence, confidentiality, communication and supervision, along with the duties of candour to a court and of charging reasonable fees. How closely a lawyer checks the output depends on the task. Document review calls for more independent checking than using a tool to generate ideas.
