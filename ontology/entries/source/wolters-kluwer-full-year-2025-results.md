## Citation

Wolters Kluwer, "2025 Full-Year Results", investor presentation dated 25 February 2026, published as a PDF on the company's content hub. It is the company's own account of its results.

## What it establishes

Wolters Kluwer reported 2025 revenues of €6,125 million, up 6% on an organic basis (growth that excludes acquisitions and currency effects). Recurring revenues made up 83% of the total. The Legal and Regulatory division reported revenues of €1,005 million, up 5% organically, and an adjusted operating margin of 18.2%. The division has two parts, Legal and Regulatory Information Solutions and Legal and Regulatory Software. The presentation names the Enterprise Legal Management (ELM) software as TyMetrix 360° and Passport and the practice management software as mainly Kleos and Legisway. It lists the acquisition of Brightflag in June 2025, which expanded the ELM business, under the software part, and the acquisition of the Libra AI Assistant under the information part. It also names Stacey Caywood as designated chief executive, succeeding the retiring Nancy McKinstry.
