## Citation

Wendy J. Muchman, "Generative Artificial Intelligence Tools: ABA Formal Opinion 512 Provides Needed Guidance on the Benefits and Burdens of Lawyers' Use of GAI", The Bar Examiner, volume 93, number 3, Fall 2024. The National Conference of Bar Examiners publishes the journal. The American Bar Association issued Formal Opinion 512 on 29 July 2024. The opinion itself is a separate source entry. Its PDF refuses scripted fetches, so The Contract Room relies on this analysis for what the opinion says.

## What it establishes

Formal Opinion 512 applies the ABA's Model Rules of Professional Conduct to generative AI. The article says the ABA's ethics committee issues its opinions to guide courts, lawyers and the public. A lawyer who uses an AI tool keeps the duties of competence, confidentiality, communication and supervision. How much a lawyer must check the output depends on the task. Document review needs more independent checking than brainstorming. The analysis quotes the opinion's warning against "uncritical reliance on content created by a GAI tool". Firms must also set policies and train staff on the tools. The opinion is on this list because it states, for US lawyers, why a person still reviews what an agent finds against the playbook.
