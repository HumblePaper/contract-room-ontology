## Citation

CLOC, "2025 CLOC State of the Industry Report: 83% of Legal Departments Face Rising Demand and AI Adoption Nearly Doubles", 12 February 2025. CLOC is the Corporate Legal Operations Consortium, a membership body for legal operations staff. The data comes from Harbor's 21st annual Law Department Survey, run with CLOC.

## What it establishes

The survey covered 186 organisations in 14 countries and more than 15 industries. In it, "83% of legal departments expect demand to increase". 63% named workload and resource bandwidth as their top challenge. The report states the figure as demand for legal work in general, not contract volume in particular. It also does not say that resources are flat. The Contract Room cites it for the gap between rising demand and the capacity to meet it, which is the case for applying a playbook consistently and at speed.
