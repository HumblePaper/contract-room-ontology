## Citation

Mansi Rana, "AI-Powered Clause Intelligence and Playbook Automation in Modern CLM", Legistify blog, updated on 9 September 2026. The page gives no first publication date. Legistify, an Indian company, sells contract management software with these features, so this is vendor marketing.

## What it establishes

The article describes clause intelligence: software that finds each clause in a contract, classifies it and checks it against the organisation's expected terms. It then applies the approved position or fallback wherever it finds that type of clause. The case it makes is consistency across volume. It gives the example of a team "manually re-drafting the same clause response 200 times a year across similar deals." The claim is that automation applies the same standard to all of them, without depending on each lawyer's reading. The piece is on this list for that argument about consistency at volume. Its performance claims are the vendor's own.
