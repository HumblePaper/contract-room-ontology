## Citation

Bernise Carolino, "The Association of Corporate Counsel honours memory of deceased founding member, Carl Liggio, Sr.", Canadian Lawyer, 3 February 2021. Canadian Lawyer is a Canadian legal news magazine.

## What it establishes

On 11 March 1982, Carl Liggio, then general counsel of Arthur Young & Company, met seven other general counsel in Dallas, Texas, to discuss an organisation for in-house counsel. Canadian Lawyer reports that this date marked the official birth of the American Corporate Counsel Association, whose first board was those eight founding members. In 2003 the association changed its name to the Association of Corporate Counsel to reflect its global interests and membership. The report names Veta Richardson as ACC's president and chief executive officer.
