## Citation

OpenAI, "Our structure", openai.com/our-structure, read in a browser on 15 October 2026. The page refuses scripted fetches. It describes a structure announced on 28 October 2025.

## What it establishes

OpenAI says it was founded in 2015 as a nonprofit and created a for-profit arm in 2019. Under the structure announced on 28 October 2025, the nonprofit became the OpenAI Foundation and the for-profit became OpenAI Group PBC, a public benefit corporation (a company that must pursue a stated mission alongside profit). The Foundation controls the Group and appoints its board. At closing, the page says, the Foundation held 26%, Microsoft about 27%, and employees and investors the remaining 47%.
