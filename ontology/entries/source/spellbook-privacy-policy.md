## Citation

Spellbook, "Privacy Policy", spellbook.com/legal/privacy-policy, updated 4 June 2026.

## What it establishes

The policy says Dialog Enterprises Inc. carries on business as Spellbook. The entry relies on it for that legal name only. The policy covers how Spellbook handles personal information collected through its website and software. It does not give a headquarters address, and it says nothing about funding or customers.
