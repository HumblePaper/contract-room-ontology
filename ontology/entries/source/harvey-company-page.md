## Citation

Harvey, "Company", harvey.ai/company, read on 15 October 2026. The page prints no date. Harvey wrote it about itself, so its figures are the company's own claims.

## What it establishes

The page describes Harvey as a platform for legal and professional services work, including contract analysis, due diligence, compliance and litigation. It claims more than 3,500 customers in more than 85 countries and more than 80 AmLaw 100 firms as users. It lists products that include Agents, Vault (document storage and bulk analysis), Knowledge (research), Contract Intelligence and Intake. It names its backers, among them Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, a16z, Coatue, GIC, Lightspeed and the OpenAI Startup Fund.
