## Citation

Association of Corporate Counsel, the advocacy page at advocacy.acc.com, as captured by the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine on 25 December 2016 and read on 15 October 2026. The live acc.com site refuses scripted reads, so this entry uses the archived copy that Wikipedia's article on the association cites.

## What it establishes

ACC's own advocacy page lists its key campaigns, among them the right to practise (for example an amicus brief against discrimination toward nonresident attorneys), attorney-client privilege, gatekeeper liability, and compliance and ethics. It reports the association filing amicus briefs and letters to courts, bar regulators and the OECD on behalf of in-house lawyers. It mentions work done through ACC chapters and committees. The site menu names the ACC Docket and the ACC Annual Meeting.
