## Purpose

A master services agreement lets the parties negotiate the legal terms of a continuing relationship once. They settle liability, intellectual property, data protection and termination in the master agreement, then place individual orders or statements of work under it. That saves time on every later purchase and keeps the allocation of risk the same across them.

## Anatomy

A master services agreement usually covers definitions, the ordering process, the services and service levels, and the parties' obligations. It then sets out fees and payment, intellectual property, confidentiality, data protection, warranties, indemnities, limitation of liability and insurance. It ends with term and termination, what happens on termination, and general provisions such as assignment, notices, governing law and dispute resolution. Order forms or statements of work are attached or signed separately. An order of precedence clause says which document wins if they conflict.

## Clauses it carries

The limitation of liability clause and the indemnity clause carry most of the risk, and every master services agreement contains both. The other clauses negotiated most often are the warranties, the data protection terms, and the service levels and credits. So are the terms on who owns intellectual property, the termination rights and the order of precedence.

## How it is negotiated

The party whose form is used has an advantage, because the other side must justify every change. Small customers accept the vendor's master services agreement with few changes. Enterprise customers usually make the vendor work from the customer's form. Each side reviews the other's markup against its playbook and prepares an issue list. The parties then negotiate the open points over several rounds, usually ending with a call on the last commercial points, such as the cap.

## Signing and after

Authorised signatories on both sides usually sign a master services agreement electronically. After signing, each party's legal or contract management team records the key terms, such as the renewal date, the notice periods and the cap. Later orders and amendments then stay consistent with them.

## By jurisdiction

### England and Wales

The Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 applies to an English-law master services agreement where it excludes or limits liability for negligence, or where one party contracts on the other's written standard terms of business. Parties commonly exclude the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.

### India

An Indian-law master services agreement attracts stamp duty under the relevant state stamp law. Parties usually provide for arbitration seated in an Indian city or, for cross-border agreements, in Singapore or London.

## By industry

### SaaS

In SaaS the master services agreement is often called a master subscription agreement. It sits alongside a data processing agreement, a service level agreement, and order forms that set the subscription term and fees.
