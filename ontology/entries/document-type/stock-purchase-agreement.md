## Purpose

A stock purchase agreement records the terms on which shares change hands. In a venture financing, the company issues new preferred shares to investors under a stock purchase agreement, often based on the NVCA model. In an acquisition by share purchase, the existing shareholders sell their shares to a buyer under a stock purchase agreement. English and Indian practice calls it a share purchase agreement.

## Anatomy

A stock purchase agreement starts with the agreement to buy and sell, the price and any price adjustment, and the closing mechanics. It sets the conditions to closing, the representations and warranties given by the company or the sellers, and the warranties given by the buyer. It then covers the promises that apply between signing and closing, indemnities, limits on claims, termination rights and general provisions. An acquisition agreement adds a disclosure schedule against the warranties, and often an escrow or holdback (part of the price kept back until a condition is met) for claims.

## Clauses it carries

A financing stock purchase agreement carries the company's representations and warranties, the closing conditions and the investors' duty to pay in. Its remedies are usually light. An acquisition stock purchase agreement carries extensive warranties and an indemnity for specific known risks. Its limitation of liability section sets thresholds, a cap and time limits for warranty claims, and it often includes a purchase price adjustment.

## How it is negotiated

A financing stock purchase agreement is usually negotiated from the term sheet on the NVCA model. Which side drafts it varies by market. In Silicon Valley practice, company counsel commonly drafts the definitive documents. In East Coast practice the convention varies, and the lead investor's counsel often drafts while company counsel marks up. In an acquisition, the buyer's counsel usually drafts in a negotiated sale and the seller's counsel drafts in an auction. Negotiation centres on the warranties, the disclosure, the indemnities, the cap and the conditions.

## Signing and after

A financing stock purchase agreement usually signs and closes at the same time. In the US, filings such as the amended charter and Form D follow. An acquisition stock purchase agreement may sign and close at once, or there may be a gap for regulatory approvals. At completion the parties exchange closing deliverables such as share transfer forms and resignations.

## By jurisdiction

### Delaware

Delaware law is commonly chosen for stock purchase agreements involving Delaware companies. Section 8106(c) of Title 10 of the Delaware Code lets the parties set survival periods of up to twenty years for warranty claims.

### England and Wales

In England and Wales the document is called a share purchase agreement. The seller's protections usually sit in a limitations schedule with a de minimis threshold (a floor for each claim), a basket (a floor for all claims together) and a cap.

### India

In India the document is called a share purchase agreement or a share subscription agreement. Transfers involving non-residents must follow the pricing and reporting rules under the Foreign Exchange Management Act 1999.

## By industry

### SaaS

A stock purchase agreement for a SaaS company carries detailed warranties on who owns the intellectual property, use of open source software, data protection compliance and customer contracts. The buyer's diligence centres on the limitation of liability terms that the company has agreed with its largest customers.
