## Remit

The legal function advises the company on the law that applies to its business. It negotiates and approves the company's contracts, supports its financings and acquisitions, and manages its disputes. It also keeps the company's corporate records and governance in order. The general counsel leads the legal function, which may include commercial counsel, corporate counsel, privacy counsel, contracts managers and paralegals.

## Deal-making role

The legal function sets the company's standard terms and playbooks (its approved positions and fallbacks). It negotiates commercial agreements with customers and suppliers and approves departures from the standard positions. On financings and acquisitions, it manages outside counsel. In a company that signs many sales contracts, the legal function's speed and consistency have a direct effect on revenue.

## Interfaces

The legal function works most closely with sales, which owns the customer relationship and the timetable, and with finance, which sets payment terms and approves financial exposure. It also works with procurement on supplier contracts, with security and privacy on data protection terms, and with the executive team and the board on major transactions.
