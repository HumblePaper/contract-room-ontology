## Profile

LexisNexis sells legal research, news and business information, and AI tools built on that content. It is a division of RELX, an information and analytics group, and has its headquarters in New York City. LexisNexis says it has 11,900 staff and customers in more than 150 countries. For contract work, its Lexis+ AI product and the Protégé assistant inside it draft and analyse legal documents against the LexisNexis legal database.

## What it sells

LexisNexis sells Lexis+ AI, its AI research and drafting product, and Protégé, the AI assistant inside it. LawSites reported in December 2025 that Protégé runs in two modes. Protégé Legal AI is tuned for legal drafting and analysis. Protégé General AI gives users general-purpose models from OpenAI and Anthropic, among others, working over the customer's own documents, the open web and LexisNexis content. The same report said Protégé General AI went into customer preview in August 2025 and on sale in October 2025. LexisNexis withdrew from every task except legal research in the Vals Legal AI Report of February 2025, so its tools do not appear in that report's redlining results.

## Funding and ownership

LexisNexis is a division of RELX, formerly Reed Elsevier. RELX's other divisions include Elsevier and LexisNexis Risk Solutions.
