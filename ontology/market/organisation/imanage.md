## Profile

iManage sells document and email management software. Law firms, corporate legal departments and other professional firms use it to store, find and secure their files. The company is iManage LLC, headquartered in Chicago, with development and support centres in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, India and Australia. It is privately held, with Bain Capital Tech Opportunities as a minority investor. For contract work, its document system is where a firm that uses iManage keeps its drafts and signed agreements.

## What it sells

iManage sells a cloud platform built around its document and email management system. Around it sit products for security and governance, risk and compliance, knowledge search, legal transaction management, and task and workflow management, plus iManage AI. On its own company page, iManage claims more than one million professionals as users at 4,000 organisations, and 2,500 law firms on its platform. It also claims that 81% of AmLaw 200 firms use it. Those figures are the company's own.

## Funding and ownership

Legal IT Insider reported in April 2023 that Bain Capital Tech Opportunities made a substantial minority investment in iManage. The stake is non-controlling, and the price was not disclosed. The report named Neil Araujo as chief executive and said a Bain partner joined the iManage board.
