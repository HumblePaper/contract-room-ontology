## Profile

Microsoft sells software and cloud services, including the Microsoft 365 suite and its Copilot AI assistant. Microsoft Corporation was founded in 1975 and has its headquarters in Redmond, Washington. Its shares have been publicly traded since March 1986. Satya Nadella has been chief executive since February 2014. For contract work, the relevant products are Word, with its compare and tracked-changes features, and the Legal Agent that Microsoft built into Word.

## What it sells

Microsoft sells Word as part of Microsoft 365. Word's legal blackline option compares two versions of a document and shows the differences as tracked changes in a third document. Non-Billable reported in April 2026 that Microsoft launched a contract review Legal Agent inside Word. The agent reviews a contract clause by clause against a team's playbook (its written negotiating positions), compares versions, flags risks and proposes edits. Formatting and tracked changes stay within Word. The report linked the launch to Microsoft's hire of an 18-person team from the legal AI start-up Robin earlier in 2026.

## Funding and ownership

Microsoft is a listed company. Its own fact sheet gives a worldwide headcount of 223,000 at 30 September 2026. OpenAI's structure page says that after OpenAI's October 2025 recapitalisation, Microsoft held about 27% of OpenAI Group PBC.
