## Profile

Anthropic builds the Claude family of AI models and sells them through its own apps and to developers. It calls itself an AI safety and research company. It is a public benefit corporation (a company that must pursue a stated purpose alongside profit), and its board is elected by stockholders and by a Long-Term Benefit Trust. KTVU, a San Francisco broadcaster, describes it as San Francisco-based. Anthropic was privately held at the time of writing and had filed to list its shares. Its legal plugins, its connectors to legal software and Claude for Word are the parts that reach contract work.

## What it sells

Anthropic's products include Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork (a desktop agent) and Claude for Microsoft 365. LawSites reported in May 2026 that Anthropic released more than 20 connectors linking Claude to legal software and 12 plugins for practice areas, including commercial and corporate work. The contract and document connectors included Ironclad, DocuSign, iManage and NetDocuments. The release built on a first legal plugin for Cowork in February 2026, which offered general contract review. LawSites also reported that Claude for Word started in beta in April 2026, with contract review as its first example.

## Funding and ownership

KTVU reported on 1 June 2026 that Anthropic had filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission for an initial public offering. It reported that a recent US$65 billion private round had valued Anthropic at US$965 billion. Reuters reported in September 2026 that Anthropic's prospectus warns that the limits on its liability in its contracts may not be "enforceable or adequate" against claims over the actions of autonomous AI agents.
