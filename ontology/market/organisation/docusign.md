## Profile

Docusign sells electronic signature software and, increasingly, software to manage agreements before and after signing. It is based in San Francisco. It was founded in 2003, and its shares trade on Nasdaq under the symbol DOCU. Docusign says it has more than 1.9 million paying customers. For contract work, its Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM) line is used to create, sign and manage agreements. The National CIO Review reported in March 2026 that Docusign's agreement repository, Navigator, held more than 200 million agreements.

## What it sells

Docusign's products include eSignature, Intelligent Agreement Management, contract lifecycle management (software that tracks a contract from request to renewal), Iris, which analyses agreement data, and electronic notarisation. It built its contract management business partly by buying companies. TechCrunch reported in May 2024 that Docusign was buying Lexion for US$165 million, after buying SpringCM in July 2018 for US$220 million and Seal Software in February 2020 for US$188 million.

## Funding and ownership

Docusign is a listed company. The National CIO Review reported in March 2026 that Docusign ended fiscal 2026 with annual recurring revenue of US$3.3 billion, up 8%. The same report said Intelligent Agreement Management passed US$350 million of annual recurring revenue, 10.8% of the company total, after just over 18 months on sale.
