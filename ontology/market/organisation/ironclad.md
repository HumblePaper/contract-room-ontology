## Profile

Ironclad sells contract lifecycle management software: one system that takes a contract from request and drafting through negotiation and signature to storage and later analysis. Its customers are legal teams and the business teams that buy and sell under contract. The lawyer Jason Boehmig and the engineer Cai Wangwilt founded it in 2014. It came out of Y Combinator and opened its first headquarters in San Francisco in 2015. Ironclad is privately held and backed by venture investors. Dan Springer, a former Docusign chief executive, became its chief executive in 2025.

## What it sells

Ironclad sells one contracting platform with AI built in. In October 2026 it announced Ironclad Agent, which lets legal, procurement and sales teams search and act on their contracts in plain language. It also announced a Contract Knowledge Graph, which links clauses, obligations and past decisions across a company's contracts so the agent can draw on them. On its About page, Ironclad claims more than two billion contracts processed on its platform. That figure is the company's own. Lets Data Science reported in October 2026, citing Seeking Alpha, that OpenAI was working with Ironclad to train and test AI agents, with contract lifecycle work as the focus. The commercial terms were not disclosed.

## Funding and ownership

SiliconANGLE reported in January 2022 that Ironclad raised US$150 million in a Series E round at a US$3.2 billion valuation, led by Franklin Templeton. Accel, Sequoia Capital and BOND were among the other investors that took part. The report put Ironclad's total funding at US$333 million. Ironclad's own timeline says Accel led its Series A in 2017.
