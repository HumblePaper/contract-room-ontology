## Profile

vLex sells legal research and the Vincent AI assistant to lawyers. Lluis and Angel Faus founded it in Barcelona in 2000. It combined with the legal research company Fastcase in 2023. Clio, the law practice software company, completed its purchase of vLex on 10 November 2025, and vLex now presents itself as part of Clio. For contract work, the useful product is Vincent, an AI tool for legal research and analysis that draws on the combined vLex and Fastcase legal database.

## What it sells

vLex sells Vincent, its AI tool for legal research and analysis. It also sells access to the vLex Library, the Fastcase Library, Docket Alarm and vLex Labs. The ABA Journal reported in November 2025 that Clio would combine Vincent with its own practice software into what it calls the Intelligent Legal Work Platform.

## Funding and ownership

vLex is owned by Clio. The ABA Journal reported in November 2025 that Clio completed the US$1 billion purchase on 10 November 2025. The same report said Clio announced that day that it was valued at US$5 billion after raising US$500 million in a Series G round led by New Enterprise Associates.
