## Profile

NetDocuments sells cloud document and email management software. Its customers are law firms of every size, in-house legal departments, government bodies and universities. The company was founded in 1999 and is based in Lehi, Utah. It is privately held: Warburg Pincus bought Clearlake Capital's interest in 2021, and Cove Hill Partners kept its stake. For contract work, the document system is where a firm that uses NetDocuments keeps its drafts and signed agreements. Its AI assistant and tabular review work on those documents.

## What it sells

NetDocuments sells a cloud document management system with email filing by client and matter. Around it sit Microsoft Teams integration, document bundling, eSignature, ethical walls (rules that block staff from a matter where there is a conflict) and data loss controls. Its AI products include a legal AI assistant, an AI app builder and tabular review, which asks the same questions across many documents and returns the answers in a table. On its home page, NetDocuments claims more than 7,000 customers worldwide. That figure is the company's own.

## Funding and ownership

NetDocuments announced in May 2021 that Warburg Pincus had agreed to buy the interest of Clearlake Capital Group, with Cove Hill Partners staying on. The company did not give a price. LawSites reported the same day, citing Legal IT Insider and an unnamed source, that the deal valued NetDocuments at US$1.4 billion. Chief executive Josh Baxter declined to confirm that figure.
