## Profile

Litera sells software for legal drafting, document comparison, contract review, knowledge management and business development to law firms and legal departments. It is based in Chicago. The private equity firm Hg first invested in 2019, led a further majority investment in November 2021 and still lists Litera in its portfolio. For contract work, Litera's products cover drafting, comparing versions and reviewing contracts. Litera says it serves 99% of the Am Law 100 (the hundred largest US law firms by revenue).

## What it sells

Litera groups its products under the practice of law, the business of law and corporate legal teams. Its products include Lito, which it calls a legal AI agent, and Litera Draft, and they work inside Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. It has brought in Kira and Transact on the practice side, and Foundation and Clocktimizer on the business side. The company says it has more than 15,000 customers and more than 1 million daily users.

## Funding and ownership

Litera is privately held. Hg announced a further majority investment on 5 November 2021, on undisclosed terms. Hg said then that Litera had made 12 acquisitions since 2019 and was approaching 1,000 employees in 17 countries. Litera says Avaneesh Marwaha was appointed chief executive in October 2024, having held the role from 2016 to 2022.
