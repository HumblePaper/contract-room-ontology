## Profile

Cooley is a US law firm that, by its own timeline, has advised technology companies since the 1950s and life sciences companies since the 1980s. It has its headquarters in Palo Alto, California, and was founded in San Francisco in 1920. The firm says it has nearly 1,400 lawyers in 19 offices across the United States, Asia and Europe. It has more than 400 partners in a single-tier partnership (one class of partner, all sharing in profits) and a total workforce of more than 3,000. For contract work, Cooley acts on M&A, life sciences licensing and collaboration deals, and investment fund closings.

## What it sells

Cooley's practices are corporate, litigation, regulatory and intellectual property. The firm says it has handled more than 1,600 M&A transactions worth more than US$750 billion since 2021, and calls itself the leading firm for technology and life sciences IPOs. Its timeline says it took Genentech and Amgen public in the 1980s. A Google Cloud press release of October 2026 said Cooley was the launch partner for an AI agent, still in development, that proposes redactions in court filings. Lawyers decide every redaction before filing.
