## Profile

Spellbook sells AI software that drafts and reviews contracts directly inside Microsoft Word. Its customers are law firms and in-house legal teams. The company behind it is Dialog Enterprises Inc., which carries on business as Spellbook. Bloomberg described it in October 2025 as a Toronto-based start-up. Spellbook's own pages do not state a headquarters. It is privately held and backed by venture investors. Contract work is the whole of its product, so every part of it reaches deal makers.

## What it sells

Spellbook sells a Word add-in for contract work. Its product menu lists Review, Draft, Compare, Ask, Playbooks and Associate, plus ACM, its autonomous contract management product. Its playbook feature checks a contract against a team's preferred positions and fallbacks (the concessions the team will accept). On its About page, Spellbook says the product runs on OpenAI's GPT-5 and other large language models. It claims more than 5,000 legal teams as customers in more than 80 countries. That figure is the company's own.

## Funding and ownership

Bloomberg reported in October 2025 that Spellbook raised US$50 million in a round led by Keith Rabois of Khosla Ventures, at a valuation of US$350 million. Threshold Ventures and the existing investor Inovia Capital also took part.
