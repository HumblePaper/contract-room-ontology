## Profile

Clio sells cloud software that law firms of every size use to run their practice. Since buying vLex in 2025, it also sells legal research and vLex's Vincent AI assistant. Clio's About page gives the company's name as Themis Solutions Inc. Its own site places its headquarters in Vancouver, Canada. LawSites describes it as based in Burnaby, British Columbia. Jack Newton and Rian Gauvreau founded it in 2008. Clio is privately held. Its reach into contract work comes mainly through the vLex research content and the Vincent assistant.

## What it sells

Clio calls its product the Intelligent Legal Work Platform. LawSites reported that Clio started in 2008 as cloud practice management software for small firms. On its own About page, Clio claims more than 400,000 legal professionals as users in more than 130 countries, and more than 2,000 employees. In September 2026, LawSites reported that Clio bought Learned Hand, a start-up that builds AI for judges and courts, on undisclosed terms. LawSites said the deal gives Clio's new judiciary business a product already in use.

## Funding and ownership

LawSites reported in November 2025 that Clio completed its US$1 billion purchase of vLex. In the same announcement, Clio closed a US$500 million Series G round and a US$350 million debt facility, which valued the combined company at US$5 billion. After the deal, Clio said it had US$400 million in annual recurring revenue. That figure is the company's own.
