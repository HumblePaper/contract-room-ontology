## Profile

Synopsys, Inc. supplies electronic design automation software (tools for designing chips) and semiconductor intellectual property, which are used to design and verify integrated circuits. Synopsys is a Delaware corporation with its headquarters in Sunnyvale, California. Its common stock trades on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol SNPS.

## Deal activity

Synopsys's largest transaction is its purchase of ANSYS, Inc., agreed on 15 January 2024 and completed on 17 July 2025, as reported in its filings on EDGAR.

## How it contracts

Synopsys reports its material definitive agreements on Form 8-K and files them as exhibits on EDGAR. Its merger agreement with Ansys chose Delaware law and the Delaware Court of Chancery.
