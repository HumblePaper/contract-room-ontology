## Profile

Google makes hundreds of products, from Google Search and YouTube to Android and Gmail. Its AI assistant is called Gemini. It is part of Alphabet. Larry Page and Sergey Brin started it at Stanford University, and Google Inc. was incorporated in August 1998. Its headquarters, the Googleplex, is in Mountain View, California. For contract work, two parts of Google count. Google Cloud sells Gemini Enterprise for Legal to law firms and legal teams. Google Workspace sells Docs, with comments, version history and eSignature.

## What it sells

Reuters reported in August 2026 that Google added tools for lawyers to its Gemini Enterprise platform, under the name Gemini Enterprise for Legal. Google said firms can choose among the AI models on the platform, and that it connects to Thomson Reuters, Harvey, Legora and other legal systems. Weil Gotshal, Cleary Gottlieb, Freshfields and Williams & Connolly were named among the firms working with Google on it. In October 2026, the law firm Cooley announced, in a release on Google Cloud's press site, that it was developing an agent with Google that proposes redactions in court filings. Lawyers make every final decision.

Google Docs, part of Workspace, lets a team draft agreements, request signatures and manage contract templates with eSignature in Docs. Reviewers can leave comments without leaving the document, and an earlier version can be restored at any time. Docs can also edit Microsoft Word files directly.

## Funding and ownership

Google is owned by Alphabet. Reuters describes it as "Alphabet's Google". This entry does not record Alphabet's listing or financial figures, because no Alphabet page was read for it.
