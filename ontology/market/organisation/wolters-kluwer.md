## Profile

Wolters Kluwer sells information and software to professionals in law, tax, accounting, health and compliance. Its global headquarters is in Alphen aan den Rijn, in the Netherlands, and its shares trade on Euronext Amsterdam under the symbol WKL. The group took its present form in 1987, when Kluwer and Wolters Samsom merged. Its Legal and Regulatory division sells to law firms and legal departments.

## What it sells

The Legal and Regulatory division has two parts. Legal and Regulatory Information Solutions sells digital information solutions and services. Legal and Regulatory Software sells Enterprise Legal Management (ELM) software, which the results presentation names as TyMetrix 360° and Passport, and practice management software, mainly Kleos and Legisway. The presentation says Wolters Kluwer bought Brightflag in June 2025, which expanded the ELM business, and bought the Libra AI Assistant for the information business.

## Funding and ownership

Wolters Kluwer is a listed company. Its own results presentation reported 2025 revenues of €6,125 million, up 6% on an organic basis (growth that excludes acquisitions and currency effects). The Legal and Regulatory division reported €1,005 million of that, up 5% organically. Stacey Caywood is the designated chief executive, succeeding Nancy McKinstry.
