## Profile

ANSYS, Inc. makes engineering simulation software. It is a Delaware corporation with its headquarters in Canonsburg, Pennsylvania. Ansys was listed on the Nasdaq under the symbol ANSS until Synopsys bought it. Since 17 July 2025 it has been a wholly owned subsidiary of Synopsys.

## Deal activity

Ansys's most significant transaction is its sale to Synopsys under the merger agreement dated 15 January 2024. Ansys shareholders approved the deal, and it completed on 17 July 2025.

## How it contracts

As a listed company, Ansys reported its material agreements on Form 8-K. Its merger agreement with Synopsys set a termination fee of 950 million US dollars, payable by Ansys in specified circumstances, including a change in its board's recommendation.
