## Profile

Vals AI tests AI models on professional work. It builds benchmarks for legal, financial, engineering and medical tasks and scores the models against them. Its test sets stay private, so a model cannot be trained on the answers. Pulse 2.0 reported in August 2026 that model cards from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and xAI have cited its results. For contract work, its Vals Legal AI Report measured legal AI tools against lawyers on tasks that included redlining, and the report describes itself as the first study of its kind.

## What it sells

Vals AI sells benchmarks and the infrastructure to run them. Its legal study is the Vals Legal AI Report of 27 February 2025, produced in partnership with Legaltech Hub. That report measured four legal AI tools against a group of lawyers on seven tasks. The best tool beat the lawyers on answering questions about a document. On redlining, the lawyers scored 79.7% and the best tool 65.0%. The report notes that Vals AI has a customer relationship with one or more of the vendors it tested.

## Funding and ownership

Vals AI is privately held. Pulse 2.0 reported in August 2026 that Vals AI raised a US$40 million Series A at a US$400 million valuation, led by Andreessen Horowitz. The existing investors 8VC and Bloomberg Beta took part. According to the company, revenue has grown eightfold compared with all of 2025.
