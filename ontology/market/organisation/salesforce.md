## Profile

Salesforce sells customer relationship management software (the systems sales and service teams use to track customers), along with Slack and Agentforce, its platform for AI agents. Salesforce, Inc. opened in 1999 and reports its results from San Francisco. Its shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol CRM. For contract work, two things count. Docusign's agreement platform now runs inside Salesforce, so sales teams can request and route contracts there. And Salesforce's own legal team is a named buyer of legal AI.

## What it sells

Salesforce sells its customer software as a cloud service, with Agentforce agents, Slack, Tableau and products for sales, service and commerce. In October 2026, Docusign announced that its Intelligent Agreement Management platform now runs natively in Salesforce, Agentforce and Slack. Docusign says a seller can request a contract, pull in live customer and pricing data, apply approved terms and route it to legal in one Slack thread. Legal and contract teams can review contracts and track renewals without leaving Salesforce. Those descriptions are Docusign's own.

In September 2026, Legora announced that Salesforce had rolled out Legora across its Legal and Corporate Affairs organisation in North America, EMEA and APAC, alongside its own Agentforce and Slack. Erica Swainson, Salesforce's vice president for legal innovation and strategic initiatives, was quoted on using it for research and drafting.

## Funding and ownership

Salesforce is a listed company. In its results release of 25 February 2026, it reported revenue of US$41.5 billion for the fiscal year ended 31 January 2026, up 10% on the year before.
