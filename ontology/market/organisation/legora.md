## Profile

Legora sells AI software for legal work. Lawyers use it for research, document review and drafting across large matters. The company behind it is Legora AB, a Swedish company with its address in Stockholm. Legora is privately held and backed by venture investors. Max Junestrand is its chief executive and a co-founder. Its customers are law firms and in-house legal teams, and its review and drafting tools are the parts of the product that reach contract work.

## What it sells

Legora sells one platform, which it calls an agentic operating system for legal work. The platform runs research, review and drafting tasks on a team's documents. On its own About page, Legora claims more than 130,000 legal professionals as users, at more than 2,000 law firms and in-house teams in more than 80 markets. In September 2026, Legora announced that Salesforce had rolled out the platform across its legal organisation in North America, EMEA and APAC.

## Funding and ownership

Legal IT Insider reported in March 2026 that Legora raised US$550 million in a Series D round led by Accel, at a valuation of US$5.55 billion. Existing investors Benchmark, Bessemer Venture Partners, General Catalyst, ICONIQ, Redpoint Ventures and Y Combinator took part. New investors included Bain Capital, Menlo Ventures and Salesforce Ventures.
