## Profile

Luminance sells AI that reads, negotiates and stores contracts for legal teams and the rest of the business. Luminance says it was born out of the University of Cambridge. It lists offices in London, Cambridge, New York, San Francisco, Dallas, Toronto, Vancouver and Sydney. TECHi reported in February 2025 that Eleanor Lightbody, the chief executive, took over from the founders after the Series A round. For contract work, Luminance covers negotiation, the contract repository and contract workflows, and it sells to procurement, sales and finance teams as well as lawyers.

## What it sells

Luminance's products are Negotiation, Repository and Workflows, and it lists technology it calls Luna. The company says it has more than 1,000 customers in more than 70 countries. In February 2025, TECHi reported that it had more than 700 clients, including AMD, Hitachi, LG Chem, SiriusXM, Rolls-Royce and Lamborghini.

## Funding and ownership

Luminance is privately held. TECHi reported in February 2025 that Luminance raised a US$75 million Series C led by Point72 Private Investments. The round brought its total raised to US$165 million. The existing investors in that round included March Capital, National Grid Partners and the law firm Slaughter and May.
