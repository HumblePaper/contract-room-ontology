## Profile

CLOC, the Corporate Legal Operations Consortium, is a not-for-profit association for legal operations professionals. Legal operations means running a legal department as a business. CLOC was founded in 2015. It grew out of regular meetings that began when Connie Brenton, who was relocating, looked for others working in what was then a new role. CLOC lists her as one of its founding board members. CLOC says it has more than 6,300 members in more than 60 countries. For contract work, its annual State of the Industry report is a regular source on legal department budgets, staffing and AI use.

## What it sells

CLOC runs events, including the CLOC Global Institute and summits in Europe and Australia. It publishes a framework for the field called the CLOC Core 12. It also publishes an annual State of the Industry report. The 2026 edition, released on 2 March 2026, surveyed leaders of 135 legal departments. CLOC reported that 85% now have dedicated AI oversight or resources, and that only 32% expect attorney headcount to grow.

## Membership

CLOC brings together legal operations professionals, the service providers who sell to them and others with a stake in the field. The association gives its membership as more than 6,300 people in more than 60 countries.
