## Profile

Goodwin is a law firm that handles transactions, litigation and regulatory work. Robert Eliot Goodwin and Joseph Osborne Procter Jr. started it in Boston in 1912. The firm says it has more than 1,800 lawyers. The firm says its clients work in life sciences, private equity, real estate, technology and financial services. For contract work, Goodwin advises those clients on complex transactions, including M&A.

## What it sells

Goodwin sells legal advice on transactions, disputes and regulation. Bloomberg Law reported in October 2025 that M&A and litigation work lifted its revenue in the year to 30 September 2025, and that much of its litigation is intellectual property work for technology and life sciences clients.

## Funding and ownership

Goodwin Procter LLP is a limited liability partnership. Bloomberg Law reported in October 2025 that the firm's revenue reached US$2.7 billion in the year ending 30 September 2025, up more than 12%, according to its chairman, Anthony McCusker. That was more than US$1.6 million in revenue per lawyer. Bloomberg Law also reported that Goodwin ranked 18th by revenue in the Am Law 2024 list.
