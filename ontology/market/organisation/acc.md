## Profile

The Association of Corporate Counsel (ACC) is the professional body for lawyers who work inside companies rather than in law firms. Its headquarters is in Washington, D.C. Canadian Lawyer reported in February 2021 that the association was born as the American Corporate Counsel Association at a meeting of eight general counsel in Dallas on 11 March 1982. It took its present name in 2003 to reflect its global membership. It speaks for in-house lawyers on issues such as the right to practise across jurisdictions and attorney-client privilege. For contract work, its surveys are a source on the general counsel's role and on how company legal departments are run.

## What it sells

ACC publishes a magazine, ACC Docket, and holds an annual meeting. It runs a Chief Legal Officers Survey. ACC said the 2023 edition, run with Exterro, drew almost 900 general counsel and chief legal officers from 35 countries in 20 industry sectors. That edition covers the role and reach of the chief legal officer, the value of the legal department, the political and regulatory landscape and the outlook for the coming year. The full report is available only to members.

## Membership

ACC's members are lawyers who work in company legal departments. Its members meet through regional chapters and practice committees.
