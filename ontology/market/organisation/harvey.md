## Profile

Harvey sells AI software for legal and professional services work. Its customers are law firms, in-house legal teams and professional services firms. The US company is Harvey AI Corporation, with its office in San Francisco, and an Irish company serves users in the European Union. Harvey is privately held and backed by venture investors. For contract work, its Contract Intelligence product helps teams review and negotiate agreements, and its Vault product stores documents and analyses them in bulk.

## What it sells

Harvey sells a platform of connected products. Agents carry out set legal tasks from start to finish. Vault holds a team's documents and runs the same questions across many of them at once. Knowledge answers legal, regulatory and tax research questions. Contract Intelligence supports contract review and negotiation, and Intake routes requests from the business to the legal team. On its own company page, Harvey claims more than 3,500 customers in more than 85 countries, including more than 80 of the AmLaw 100 firms.

## Funding and ownership

LawSites reported in September 2026 that Harvey raised US$550 million at a US$15.5 billion valuation, in a round co-led by Diffusion and Lightspeed. LawSites put Harvey's total funding since its 2022 start at more than US$1.5 billion. Co-founder Winston Weinberg said Harvey had passed US$400 million in annual recurring revenue. That figure is the company's own. Weinberg also said Harvey would build more specialised products for contracting, litigation, deals and compliance. Harvey's own company page names Sequoia, Kleiner Perkins, a16z, GIC and the OpenAI Startup Fund among its other backers.
