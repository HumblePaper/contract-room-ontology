## Summary

Synopsys, Inc. agreed on 15 January 2024 to buy ANSYS, Inc., and the two companies announced the deal on 16 January 2024. Ansys shareholders received 197.00 US dollars in cash and 0.3450 of a share of Synopsys common stock for each Ansys share. The joint press release valued the deal at an enterprise value of about 35 billion US dollars. The purchase completed on 17 July 2025, after Ansys shareholders approved it and antitrust regulators in several jurisdictions reviewed it.

## Parties and advisers

The parties to the merger agreement were Synopsys, Inc., its wholly owned subsidiary ALTA Acquisition Corp., and ANSYS, Inc. All three are Delaware corporations. The Synopsys and Ansys joint press release names the advisers. Evercore was financial adviser and Cleary Gottlieb Steen & Hamilton LLP was legal adviser to Synopsys. Qatalyst Partners was financial adviser to Ansys, and Skadden, Arps, Slate, Meagher & Flom LLP and Goodwin Procter LLP were its legal advisers.

## Structure

Synopsys bought Ansys through a reverse triangular merger under Delaware law, in which a buyer's new subsidiary merges into the target. ALTA Acquisition Corp. merged with and into Ansys, and Ansys survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of Synopsys. This kind of merger has the same result as buying all of the target's shares. It is the usual way to buy a US public company, because once shareholders approve it by the required vote, it binds every shareholder. Synopsys obtained commitments for 16 billion US dollars of senior bridge term loans (short-term loans until long-term funding is in place) to pay the cash part of the price.

## Notable terms

The merger agreement set two termination fees, each payable only in specified circumstances. Ansys would pay 950 million US dollars in cases such as a change in the Ansys board's recommendation or acceptance of a superior offer. Synopsys would pay 1.5 billion US dollars in cases that included a failure to obtain antitrust approval. The outside date (the deadline to complete) was 15 January 2025, and it could be extended to 15 January 2026. The Synopsys and Ansys merger agreement chose Delaware law and made the Delaware Court of Chancery the forum. It also entitled the parties to specific performance (a court order to carry out the agreement).

## Documents

The Synopsys and Ansys merger agreement is filed as Exhibit 2.1 to the Synopsys Current Report on Form 8-K reporting the event of 15 January 2024. The joint press release is filed as Exhibit 99.1. Synopsys reported the completion of the purchase in a further Form 8-K in July 2025. All of these filings are available on EDGAR.
