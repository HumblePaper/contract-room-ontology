## Summary

This is a vendor press release, syndicated from Business Wire. LicenseFortress, an independent software licensing adviser, says its new division covers AI licensing assessments, contract negotiation, agent governance, embedded AI feature reviews and audit defence across vendors such as Oracle, Microsoft, IBM, SAP and VMware. It cites forthcoming research with Database Trends and Applications in which 53% of respondents said AI had made software licensing harder. Its co-founder warns that embedded features and autonomous agents can bring unexpected costs. The survey is unpublished and sponsored by the seller.

## Figures

- 53%, surveyed organisations saying AI implementation made software licensing more difficult, per LicenseFortress research
- 54%, surveyed organisations naming usage tracking as their main AI licensing concern, per the same research
