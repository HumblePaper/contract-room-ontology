## Summary

Law.com Legaltech News reports that the law firm Cooley and Google are building an AI agent for document redaction on Google's Gemini Enterprise. The article describes redaction as a use case that would require a heavy engineering lift, and says the two are co-developing the tool. The article places the project in a wider pattern of law firms working directly with large technology companies, and says it is one of several AI projects at Cooley. For a deal team, redaction is routine work in disclosure and diligence, so a firm-built agent shows where firms expect to take work in-house. The full text sits behind a free registration, so this summary rests on the opening of the piece.
