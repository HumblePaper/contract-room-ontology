## Summary

Legaltech Hub's Cheryl Wilson Griffin argues that buyers should not read a famous investor's backing as proof that a legal tech vendor is good or safe. Venture capital firms spread money across many companies expecting most to fail, which is a different appetite for risk from a law firm or legal department that wants a vendor to last. The article cites research that about 60% of venture-backed startups fail. Only the opening is free to read, so this summary covers the part the page shows.
