## Summary

This is a podcast episode from the law firm Mintz, hosted by Jennifer Rubin with guest Tali Tuchin. They say a lawyer cannot upload a complex agreement to an AI tool and expect a full answer, because the tool misses the client's goals, the relationship and the risk. They name routine NDAs, spotting departures from standard terms and quality checks as good uses. They say deal-specific timing, regulatory deadlines and trust built between negotiators stay with people. Their advice is to read every AI draft before sharing it and to challenge each provision.
