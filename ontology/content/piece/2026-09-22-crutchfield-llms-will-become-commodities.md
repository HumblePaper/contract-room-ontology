## Summary

Ken Crutchfield, writing in LawSites, compares today's language models to steel in the 19th century, which became cheap and made the industries built on it more profitable. He expects distillation and open-source models to turn LLMs into commodity inputs, with the model makers left under margin pressure. He argues that legal technology companies that build on the models will capture more of the value. He also argues that lawyers want faster deals, not a better model, so smaller specialised models may be enough. This is an opinion piece, and for a buyer it suggests the product around the model matters more than the model.
