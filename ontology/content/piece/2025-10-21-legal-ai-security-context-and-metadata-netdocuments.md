## Summary

This is a LinkedIn post by Colin S. Levy, who gives his role as General Counsel of Malbek, which he describes as CLM for Enterprise. He reports a conversation with Dan Hauck of NetDocuments and lists three takeaways: security comes first, context matters more than power, and metadata is valuable. He adds that the lawyers seeing the most success ground AI in their own documents, workflows and expertise. The post gives no figures on AI results (date approximate).
