## Summary

Artemis reports that Pillar Capital Management, a Bermuda reinsurance and insurance-linked securities manager, has adopted Ultrassure's contract intelligence platform after an evaluation. Ultrassure organises contract information, keeps links to the source documents and helps teams work together on complex contract sets. Pillar's chief underwriting officer is quoted saying Ultrassure understood the reinsurance business. The report gives no results from the deployment. Pillar manages about 4.3 billion dollars of such assets (the page does not state the currency).
