## Summary

This is a vendor profile on Legaltech Hub, built around an interview with MiAI Law's founder and chief executive, Laina Chan. It describes MiAI Law as an Australian legal AI platform combining case law research, legislative research, case summaries, contract review, and verification of documents and pleadings, built for Australian practice. The interview covers the problems it targets, its main uses and how it differs from rivals. Only the opening is free, so this summary covers that part.
