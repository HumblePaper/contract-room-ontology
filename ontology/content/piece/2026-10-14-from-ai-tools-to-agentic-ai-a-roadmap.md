## Summary

This is a sponsored piece in Artificial Lawyer by Paul Walker of iManage, a company that sells document management software. Walker argues that a firm's results with AI agents will depend more on the quality of its information systems than on the agent it buys. He sets out four stages: organise records by matter, give agents the same access limits as lawyers, deliver ranked context through standards such as Model Context Protocol, and hand over action starting with monitoring tasks. He says supervision should match risk, and client-facing work should be reviewed as a partner reviews a junior's draft.
