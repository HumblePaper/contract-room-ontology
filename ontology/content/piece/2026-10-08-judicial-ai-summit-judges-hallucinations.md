## Summary

Law.com Legaltech News reports from a summit in New York run by Thomson Reuters and Wickard AI, where judges said federal and state courts have no common approach to generative AI. A judge named in the article, Sarah Smith, said AI hallucinations go vastly unreported, even with trackers such as Damien Charlotin's database. Joshua Deahl of the D.C. Court of Appeals said he uses AI for tasks his clerks also do. The full text sits behind a free registration, so this summary rests on the opening of the piece.
