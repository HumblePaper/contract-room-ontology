## Summary

Law.com Legaltech News argues that rules on liability have not kept up with legal AI agents that act on their own. Lawyers are using agents for motion drafting, contract review, due diligence, calendar work and actions across several systems, all on client communications and financial data. The article says privilege and confidentiality rules assume a lawyer is overseeing each step, and an agent that acts alone weakens that assumption. For a deal team, the open question is who answers when an agent passes client data on, which a vendor contract should settle before the agent is switched on. The full text sits behind a free registration, so this summary rests on the opening of the piece.
