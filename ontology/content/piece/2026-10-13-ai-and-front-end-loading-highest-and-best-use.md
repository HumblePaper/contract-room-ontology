## Summary

Above the Law columnist Stephen Embry argues that early case assessment is the best use of AI in litigation. He points to the Thomson Reuters Institute's AI Guide for Litigators, which describes AI sorting large document sets, judging scope and chance of success, and finding key custodians. Clients have often refused to pay for careful early analysis, he says, and AI lowers that cost sharply. That lets lawyers produce early chronologies, issue lists and budgets on cases of any size.
