## Summary

Pulse 2.0 reports that Definely, a London legal technology company, has connected its contract review tools to ChatGPT Enterprise through OpenAI for Law, using the Model Context Protocol (a standard way for AI tools to call outside software). Lawyers can check defined terms, search a firm's precedent bank, proofread structure and generate issues lists without leaving ChatGPT. Definely's chief executive told the outlet that the bottleneck has moved from generating text to reviewing it. The article is largely the company's account of its own product.
