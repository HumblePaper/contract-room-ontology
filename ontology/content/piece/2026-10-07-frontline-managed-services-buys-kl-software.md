## Summary

Pulse 2.0 reports that Frontline Managed Services, which supports law firm technology, has acquired KL Software Technologies and KLoBot and created Frontline Intelligent Applications for law firms. The unit offers advice, professional services, managed services and a portfolio of connected legal applications including KLapper, Legal101, netDocShare and imDocShare. KL Software's founder, Ragav Jagannathan, will lead it. The article says KL Software serves more than 30 Am Law firms, and that the two companies had partnered earlier in 2026.
