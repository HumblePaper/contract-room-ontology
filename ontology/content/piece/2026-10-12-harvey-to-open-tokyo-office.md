## Summary

This is the vendor's own announcement. Harvey says it will open a Tokyo office in early 2027 to support law firms and in-house teams in Japan, where it names Mori Hamada & Matsumoto, Nagashima Ohno & Tsunematsu and Mitsubishi Corporation among its clients. It also says it is working with Legalscape on Japanese legal research. The Tokyo office joins Harvey's offices in Sydney, Singapore and Bengaluru. The customer count is Harvey's own claim.

## Figures

- 3500 plus, Harvey customers, as stated by Harvey
- 85 plus, countries where Harvey says it has customers
