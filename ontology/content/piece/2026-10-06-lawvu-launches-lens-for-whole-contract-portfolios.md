## Summary

Pulse 2.0 reports that LawVu has launched Lens, an add-on to its LegalOS product that analyses a company's contract portfolio as a whole instead of one document at a time. In-house teams ask questions in plain language and receive answers that cite the source contracts. LawVu points to uses such as spotting renewals on poor terms, finding change-of-control and consent clauses in due diligence, and checking which contracts a data breach or port closure touches. LawVu says customer data is not used to train models. The article follows the company's announcement.
