## Summary

Law.com Legaltech News reports that ArentFox Schiff has closed AFSChat, the generative AI chatbot it built in house. The firm made it as a low-risk way for lawyers and staff to try large language models. It became redundant once the firm adopted Harvey and Microsoft Copilot, which the firm found better and which met its requirements. The firm calls the closure the end of an experiment, not a failure, and a buyer can read it as a sign that a home-built tool may not outlast a commercial one. The full text sits behind a free registration, so this summary rests on the opening of the piece.
