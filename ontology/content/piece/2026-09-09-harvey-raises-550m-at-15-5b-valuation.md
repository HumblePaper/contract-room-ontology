## Summary

LawSites reports that Harvey raised US$550 million at a US$15.5 billion valuation, in a round co-led by Diffusion and Lightspeed, taking its total funding to over US$1.5 billion. Co-founder Winston Weinberg said Harvey has more than US$400 million in annual recurring revenue and over 3,000 customers, including 80% of the top 100 law firms. These are the company's own figures. It plans to spend the money on new products for contracting, litigation, deals and compliance. For contract teams, it signals that the best-funded legal AI company is moving toward their work.

- US$550 million, size of the round
- US$15.5 billion, valuation after the round
- US$400 million, annual recurring revenue, per co-founder Winston Weinberg
