## Summary

Dorine Coffin and Lance Odegard, writing for the Thomson Reuters Institute, argue that the larger risk in legal AI is not a wrong answer but a team that takes the AI's framing without testing it. They describe synthetic confidence, where each person assumes someone else checked the draft. They recommend naming one team member as challenger, asking people to state their reasoning in their own words, and returning to primary sources at decision points. Thomson Reuters sells legal AI, which is the publisher's interest in the topic.
