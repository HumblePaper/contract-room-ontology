## Summary

This is a vendor press release from Ironclad. It introduces Ironclad Agent, a chat interface for legal, procurement and sales teams to search and act on contracts, and a Contract Knowledge Graph that holds each customer's contracts, approved positions and past decisions. A Contract Review Agent redlines against those positions, and a Workflow Designer Agent builds contract processes from a conversation. Ironclad quotes an ANDMORE executive who said review time fell from three hours to about 20 minutes. The 86% figure is attributed to an IDC 2026 report, and the 88% figure is Ironclad's own.

## Figures

- 86%, organisations that found disconnected contract processes delay revenue and purchasing cycles, per an IDC 2026 report cited by Ironclad
- 88%, Ironclad customers surveyed who plan to expand their use of Ironclad's agents, per Ironclad
- 3 hours to about 20 minutes, contract review time reported by one ANDMORE executive
