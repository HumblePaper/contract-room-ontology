## Summary

LawSites reports that Repario, an e-discovery and managed review company backed by JLL Partners, has bought UnitedLex, an alternative legal services provider that CVC Capital Partners has owned since 2018. UnitedLex serves enterprise clients in legal process outsourcing, operations, litigation, IP and contracts. The combined company says it will offer AI-enhanced services and give clients control of their data, working from centres in the United States, Mexico, India, the United Kingdom and the EU. The article notes leadership turnover at UnitedLex in 2024. For a legal department, the deal means one fewer independent provider of outsourced contract work.
