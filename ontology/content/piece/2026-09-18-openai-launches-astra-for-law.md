## Summary

Reuters reports that OpenAI released Astra for Law, a version of its GPT-6 Astra model with an index of US case law, statutes and regulations and instructions for legal analysis and writing. Harvey and Legora will build products on it, and integrations are planned with Relativity, Clio, Intapp and Thomson Reuters. Selected law firms, among them Sullivan & Cromwell, Ropes & Gray, Cooley, Latham & Watkins and Wachtell Lipton, get first access under a programme with protections for client confidentiality. Reuters sets the launch against Google's Gemini Enterprise legal offerings and Anthropic's tools for lawyers.
