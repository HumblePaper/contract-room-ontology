## Summary

This is a vendor press release, carried by Sales Tech Star. Leah launched Leah Contracting on its Maestro platform and argues that contract lifecycle management (software people operate) has reached its limit. Specialist agents handle intake, review, negotiation, approval, signature, obligations and renewal, applying a company's playbooks and risk checks. Leah also offers pricing by completed workload, single-tenant deployment and a promise that customer data will not train other models. The claims are the vendor's own.
