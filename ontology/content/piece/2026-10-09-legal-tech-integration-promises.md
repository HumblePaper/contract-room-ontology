## Summary

Law.com Legaltech News reports that legal technology vendors are promoting integrations through APIs and MCP connectors (a standard that lets AI tools reach other software), but they may fail to deliver. The article says buyers sometimes find the gaps only after they have bought. Paul Gilford, chief information officer at Hogan Lovells, told the publication that integrations have improved over time while gaps remain. A failed integration can also expose firm and client data, so a buyer should test each promised connection before signing.
