## Summary

Mayer Brown's Spencer Glende and Sam Hartman set out how a buyer should structure a pilot of an autonomous AI agent before deploying it. They advise judging the pilot on three things: whether the agent can do the job, whether its safety guardrails work in practice, and whether the cost is worth it once licences, compute, API fees and human oversight are counted. The pilot agreement should fix the agent's data, systems, users and permitted actions. It should also state exit criteria, so that wider rollout needs a decision based on the pilot's evidence rather than happening by default.
