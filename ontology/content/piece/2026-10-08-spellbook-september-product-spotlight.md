## Summary

This is Spellbook's own monthly product roundup, listing eight updates for September 2026. Autonomous Contract Management became generally available, and Comprehensive Reviews entered early access, combining playbook checks, risk review and proofreading in one pass. Users can now pick specific playbook rules for a targeted review, and research agents can pull answers from several documents with citations. The rest include formatting and research-and-editing improvements in Associate, plus admin items such as a new Admin Console and audit logs. The post is a vendor's list of its own releases and is not independent.
