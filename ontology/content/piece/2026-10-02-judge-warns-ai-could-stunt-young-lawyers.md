## Summary

Hoodline, in a piece it says is derived from Reuters reporting, says US District Judge Arun Subramanian found citation errors in a copyright case, traced to AI tools from Harvey used by the firm Shapiro Arato Bach. He did not sanction the lawyers but warned that AI-generated work must be checked before it reaches a judge. He also raised the risk to clients and the loss of training for junior lawyers who rely on automated drafting. The firm called the error isolated and said the lawyer broke internal policy. The page adds survey figures, naming the American Bar Association and the North Carolina Bar Association as their sources.

## Figures

- 31%, legal professionals using generative AI at work in 2025, up from 27% in 2024, per the American Bar Association as cited by Hoodline
- 43%, firms with no formal AI policy, per the North Carolina Bar Association as cited by Hoodline
- 54%, firms giving no AI training, per the North Carolina Bar Association as cited by Hoodline
