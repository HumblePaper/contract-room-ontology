## Summary

Pulse 2.0 reports that Checkbox has launched First Pass, an AI agent that handles legal requests, contracts and invoices before a lawyer is involved. It routes requests by workload, compares incoming contracts with a company's template to return a risk score and summary, and checks LEDES invoices against billing guidelines. Checkbox says its customers cut NDAs needing legal review by up to 95%, and that more than 100 legal teams use it, including SAP and PepsiCo. Early access is open, with wider release planned for the first quarter of 2027.
