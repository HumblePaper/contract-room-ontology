## Summary

This is an opinion post on Above the Law by Joe Borstein, looking back at his 2017 prediction that legal tech founders would become billionaires by 2027. He reports that the forecast has largely come true, citing legal tech funding of US$4.6 billion in 2025, about 12,800 legal tech companies, and 16 unicorns. He also cites a rise in general counsel using generative AI from 44% to 87% in a year, while noting that only 18% of firms measure the return on AI. The figures are his own and the post is an opinion, so check them before relying on them.

## Figures

- US$4.6 billion, legal tech funding in 2025, as cited by the author
- 12800, legal tech companies worldwide, approximate number, as cited
- 16, legal tech unicorns to date, as cited
- 87%, general counsel using generative AI, up from 44% a year earlier, as cited
- 18%, firms that measure AI return on investment, as cited
- 46, Big Law lawyers who joined AI companies in the first half of 2026, as cited
