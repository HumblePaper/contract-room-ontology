## Summary

Trending Topics reports, in German, that Berlin's Noxtua closed a Series C of more than EUR 100m, and that the Munich publisher C.H.BECK became its majority shareholder, with Austria's MANZ joining as an investor. Stake sizes and valuation were not disclosed. Several earlier investors left, and CMS and Dentons remain anchor customers. The company reports more than 30,000 users and about 100 staff. The article sets this beside Harvey and Legora, whose reported valuations are far higher.

## Figures

- EUR 100m plus, Series C
- EUR 80.7m, Series B in spring 2025, led by C.H.BECK
- 30 thousand plus, Noxtua users, as reported
