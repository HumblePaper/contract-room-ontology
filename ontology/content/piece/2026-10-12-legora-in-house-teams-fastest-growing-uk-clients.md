## Summary

This is a vendor press release from Legora, with the company's own numbers. It says in-house legal teams are now just over half its UK clients, and that since 1 January 2026 their use has grown at more than three times the rate of its UK law firm clients, whose own adoption more than tripled. Banks and insurers are the largest group of in-house users, followed by professional services, and Barclays and Deloitte are named. Legora credits source citations, ethical walls, permissions and audit trails for winning regulated buyers. It also says it bought the London firm Wexler in July 2026.
