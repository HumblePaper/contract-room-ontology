## Summary

LawSites reports that OpenAI's launch of Astra for Law was followed by announcements of 26 ChatGPT plugins from legal technology vendors. OpenAI's own announcement named only four, Harvey, iManage, Legora and Thomson Reuters. Bob Ambrogi asked OpenAI for the full list without success, then collected others from vendors who wrote to him after his first article. Those include Ironclad Contracts, Litera, NetDocuments, Everlaw, Relativity, Laurel, LegalZoom and Clio's Vincent. Contract teams that already use one of these vendors may soon be able to reach it from inside ChatGPT.
