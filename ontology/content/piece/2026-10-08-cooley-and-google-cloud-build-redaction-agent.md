## Summary

This is a vendor release published by Google Cloud. It says the law firm Cooley is the launch partner for a Gemini Enterprise agent that flags personal data, technical information and other confidential material in court filings and proposes redactions. Attorneys make every final decision on what is redacted before filing. The release says the agent is meant to cut days of attorney review time, and gives Cooley's size as about 1,400 lawyers. It gives no availability date.
