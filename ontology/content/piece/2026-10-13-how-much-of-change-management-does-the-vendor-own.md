## Summary

This is a post on the site of CLOC, the Corporate Legal Operations Consortium, recording a conversation with Jerry Levine, Colin Levy and Rich Robinson. They agree that a vendor can supply training, method and governance but cannot own the buyer's transformation, which needs leadership from inside the company. They advise buyers to settle who does what on change management before signing, and to bring in an outside implementation partner if neither side has the skill. The post says legal AI adoption reached 78% by 2026 but average time saved was 18.3%, against 41% projected by vendors.

## Figures

- 78%, legal AI adoption by 2026, as stated in the post
- 18.3%, average time savings reported
- 41%, time savings projected by vendors
