## Summary

Legaltech Hub's Nabiha Khwaja reports from Nordic Legal Tech Day 2026 in Stockholm. She writes that the argument had moved on from whether the technology works, since the agents work, the tools have matured and funding is arriving. The hard conversations were about people: how to train a junior lawyer to judge an answer that looks perfect, who gets rewarded for building, and what is left for the human to do. Only the opening is free to read, so this summary covers that part.
