## Summary

This is a vendor press release from Milliman, syndicated from Business Wire. Contract Clarity for Healthcare reads pharmacy and medical contracts, flags high-risk clauses and compares terms with Milliman's best practices. Users can ask questions across several agreements and tie contract language to cost models to see the financial effect before signing. A Milliman consultant says it does in minutes what used to take his team weeks, which is a single customer view and not a measured result.
