## Summary

Canadian Lawyer reports a survey of 104 people at law firms and in-house legal teams, run for DISCO by Ari Kaplan Advisors in the summer of 2026. Among in-house respondents, 47% felt pressure from their board or senior executives to adopt legal AI, up from 18% a year before. Among law firm respondents, 64% felt that pressure from clients, up from 39%. The article also reports that legal AI pricing has moved from annual subscriptions to charges based on use. DISCO sells legal AI software and the sample is small, so the figures show a direction more than a size.

## Figures

- 47%, in-house respondents who felt board or senior executive pressure to adopt legal AI, up from 18%
- 64%, law firm respondents who felt client pressure to adopt legal AI, up from 39%
- 104, people surveyed at law firms and corporate legal departments
