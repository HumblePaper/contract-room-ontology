## Summary

This is a sponsored podcast post on Above the Law, from the Adventures in Legal Tech series hosted by Jared Correia. Rachel Shields Williams, director of AI and knowledge management at Bass, Berry & Sims, says firms are investing heavily in AI while their decades of data remain disorganised. Her line is that the real work happens before any AI tool touches the files. The point for any team building a clause library or playbook is that the tidy-up comes before the tool.
