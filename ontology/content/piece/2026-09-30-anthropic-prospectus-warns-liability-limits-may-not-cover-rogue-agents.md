## Summary

Reuters reports that Anthropic's prospectus for its planned stock market listing says it could face claims over the actions of autonomous AI agents, and that the law on this is uncertain. Anthropic says the limits on its liability in customer contracts may not be enforceable or adequate against such claims. Open questions it lists include whether an agent's action is a product or a service, and whether it can bind the user who deployed it. Reuters also quotes the FTC chairman asking whether liability should lie with the person who innocently used the tool, or with the toolmaker.
