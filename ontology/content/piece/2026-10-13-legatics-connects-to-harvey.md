## Summary

Artificial Lawyer reports that Legatics, a transaction management platform, has connected to Harvey's connector library through its Model Context Protocol server. Harvey users can now ask questions about live matter data held in Legatics, including parties, deal facts, checklist status and data room contents, across one deal or several. Legatics says its platform holds the live state of the deal, which a transactional AI cannot otherwise reach. Harvey's Nate Schlein says answers come with the firm's own permissions intact. For deal teams this ties AI answers to the current state of the deal file.
