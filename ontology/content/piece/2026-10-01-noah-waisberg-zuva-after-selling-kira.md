## Summary

LawSites publishes a LawNext podcast interview with Noah Waisberg, who sold his contract-analysis company Kira Systems to Litera five years ago. He kept the technology and about 30 of Kira's roughly 180 staff to start Zuva, which began as an API service and now helps sellers in M&A. After interviewing some 200 dealmakers what frustrates them in a sale, Zuva built software to manage questions from bidders, and it offers diligence that pairs Kira's AI with former large-firm associates at lower cost. The conversation also covers his recently expired non-compete. The sell side of a deal is a newer target for contract AI.
