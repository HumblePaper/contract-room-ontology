## Summary

Pulse 2.0 reports that TeddyHoldings.AI, incubated by the holding company Tucker's Farm, closed a US$60m seed round and says it has passed US$25m in annual revenue. It describes itself as a compliance and client-service legal platform more than an AI company. The article reports that about US$115m of possible equity was offered during a three-week raise before the round closed at US$60m. The company is keeping its leadership team and ownership details private for now.

## Figures

- US$60m, seed round
- US$25m, annual revenue the company says it has passed
- US$115m, equity commitments offered during the three-week raise, as reported
