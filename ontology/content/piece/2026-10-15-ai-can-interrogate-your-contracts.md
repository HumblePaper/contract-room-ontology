## Summary

This is a post by Catherine Carroll, head of legal operations at iManage, a company that sells document management software, in Legal Futures. She says AI agents using open standards such as the model context protocol can answer contract questions in minutes that once took weeks, but they also magnify poor data: duplicate files, inconsistent names, unclear versions and scattered storage. She sets out four steps: one governed repository, machine-readable classification, clean-up ordered by risk, and agent-specific permissions with audit trails. She also proposes a 90-day plan that starts with a security review. The advice is the author's, not a finding.
