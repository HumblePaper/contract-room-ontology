## Summary

This is a guest post by Sam Flynn, co-founder and COO of Josef, a company that sells legal automation tools, published in LawSites. Flynn says legal operations teams are moving from one contract lifecycle management (CLM) platform to a stack of separate tools for intake, redlining and storage. He gives three reasons: all-in-one platforms did a few things well and the rest poorly, a three-year commitment feels risky in a fast-moving market, and AI and connector tools now make linking systems easier. He also lists costs: harder migration, upkeep of home-built links, more security reviews and unclear responsibility when something fails.
