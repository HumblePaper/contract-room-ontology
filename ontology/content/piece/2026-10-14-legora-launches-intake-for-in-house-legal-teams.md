## Summary

This is a vendor press release. Legora's Intake collects employee requests in Slack, asks clarifying questions and prepares a first draft from the legal team's own playbooks and precedents, so a lawyer reviews instead of starting blank. The team decides what the agent may finish alone, such as a standard NDA, and what needs a lawyer before the reply goes out. Legora cites the ACC 2026 benchmarking report for a median of 367 employees per in-house lawyer, up 26% from 291 a year earlier, and says only 32% of legal departments expect to hire more attorneys. It plans to add Teams, email and MCP.
