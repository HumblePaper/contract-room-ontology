## Summary

Law.com reports that AI use at law firms is rising fast while vendors move to pricing based on use to recover their costs. Most firm AI leaders quoted are not alarmed by possible price rises, and they look at ways to use AI more efficiently. They see falling adoption as a bigger risk than higher bills. Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer's chief AI officer, Ilona Logvinova, says she would not call it concerning. The page is behind a paywall, so this summary covers only the opening of the article.
