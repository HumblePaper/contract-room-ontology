## Summary

LawSites reports that Filevine has launched an AI citator and brief-checking tool in its LOIS system. It looks for hallucinated citations and checks whether a legal point is still good law, working at the level of a passage rather than a whole case. Filevine tested it on 68 matters where courts had identified hallucinated citations tied to admitted or suspected AI use. Of 2,073 citations, it verified 61.8%, flagged 38.2% for attorney review and marked 8.4% as severe. These are the company's own results, and a citation check is a second line of defence for any team drafting with AI.

## Figures

- 68, matters with admitted or suspected AI use and court-identified hallucinated citations, used in Filevine's test
- 2073, citations analysed
