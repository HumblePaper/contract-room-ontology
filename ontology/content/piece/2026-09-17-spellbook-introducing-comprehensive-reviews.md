## Summary

This is Spellbook's own product post. Comprehensive Reviews runs a playbook check, a risk check and a proofread on a contract in one click, instead of three separate reviews. Findings that overlap appear once, and playbook failures and high-priority risks come first, with lower-priority items collapsed. The proofread covers broken references, undefined terms, placeholder text and stray comments, and results are measured against the user's own template or past contracts. The claims are the vendor's and the post offers no test results.
