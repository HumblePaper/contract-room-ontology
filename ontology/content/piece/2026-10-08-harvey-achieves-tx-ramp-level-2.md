## Summary

This is a vendor post on Harvey's own blog. Harvey says it reached Level 2 of the Texas Risk and Authorization Management Program (TX-RAMP) on 22 September 2026. Level 2 covers cloud services that handle confidential or regulated data, and the certification lets Texas state agencies, courts and public universities use Harvey for work such as contract review, compliance filings and legal research. Harvey lists its SOC 2 Type II attestation and ISO 27001, 27701 and 42001 certifications as existing credentials.
