## Summary

TechAfrica News reports that Etisalat has completed its first contract evaluation in procurement using an agentic AI solution in its private cloud. The company says the approach could cut the procurement lifecycle by about 50%, and improve productivity and consistency of decisions. It stresses that security and governance are part of the design. Etisalat plans to extend agentic AI across the whole procurement process. The 50% is the company's expectation, not a measured result.
