## Summary

LawSites reports that Clio has bought Learned Hand, an AI platform that helps judges and court clerks organise materials, flag inconsistencies and draft bench memos. Financial terms were not disclosed. The Michigan Supreme Court and trial courts in ten states already use it. The deal follows Clio's hires of Pablo Arredondo and Bridget McCormack to build a judiciary business, and it shows a practice-management vendor moving into courts. Clio's earlier vLex purchase, the article says, gives access to more than 1 billion legal documents across more than 110 jurisdictions.

## Figures

- 1 billion or more, legal documents the article says Clio can reach through vLex
- 110 or more, jurisdictions covered by those documents
- 10, US states whose trial courts use Learned Hand
