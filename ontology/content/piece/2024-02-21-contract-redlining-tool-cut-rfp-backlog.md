## Summary

This is a LinkedIn post by Aniruth Sarathy, whose headline the guest page does not show, and it is more than a year old. He says his team built a gen AI tool that finds each potential redline in a request for proposal (RFP) against a set of non-negotiable terms, and the lawyer accepts or rejects each one. He reports that it cut the RFP backlog from one month to 7 days and saves each attorney 19 hours a week. These are the author's own figures and the post gives no base for them (date approximate).
