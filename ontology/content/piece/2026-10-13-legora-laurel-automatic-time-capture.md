## Summary

LawSites reports that the legal AI platform Legora has partnered with the timekeeping software Laurel. In the first phase, Laurel turns tasks done in Legora into time entries, with client, matter and description filled in for the lawyer to review. In the second phase, the two companies will work with clients to build measures of how lawyers use Legora and what it is worth to a firm, and show them on both products. The first phase launches first, and firms using both products will get rollout details. Tying an AI tool to billing is one way a firm can show whether the tool pays for itself.
