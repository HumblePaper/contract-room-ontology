## Summary

Lets Data Science, a secondary source citing a 6 October 2026 Seeking Alpha report, says that OpenAI has partnered with the contract management company Ironclad to train and test AI agents on contract work. The programme picks high-value tasks, such as document review, clause analysis, redlining and approval routing, and turns them into research problems. The article says the models, training data, measures, commercial terms and timelines have not been disclosed. It adds that enterprise agent programmes usually need human-checked evaluations that stress accuracy against the document over generic benchmarks.
