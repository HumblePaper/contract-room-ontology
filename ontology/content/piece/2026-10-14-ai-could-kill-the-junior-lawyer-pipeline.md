## Summary

Law.com International asks what happens to training when AI takes over the research, drafting and document review through which junior lawyers learned their trade. The article poses the question of how the next generation will build skill and judgement if machines do that work, and asks whether banning AI is the answer. The page is behind a paywall, so this summary covers only the opening of the article.
