## Summary

LawSites reports that the Third Circuit sided with Thomson Reuters against ROSS Intelligence, the AI legal research company that has since shut down. The panel upheld Judge Bibas's February 2025 summary judgment that Westlaw headnotes are original enough to be copyrighted and that ROSS's copying was not fair use. The opinion was still sealed pending redaction requests when the article ran. The article calls it the first federal appeals ruling on fair use in AI training, but notes that it may have limited reach, because the copying came before generative AI took off and the system was built to compete with Westlaw.
