## Summary

LawSites reports that OpenAI has released Astra for Law, a configuration of its GPT-6 Astra model for legal work, with legal instructions and tools and a Legal Search Index of over 230 million URLs of case law, statutes and regulations. Am Law 200 firms can join a Trusted Access Program with zero data retention on the API, and ChatGPT Enterprise usage is excluded from human review. OpenAI reports the model scored 54% correct on 200 legal research questions in a Vals AI Legal Research Bench test, against 38.7% for GPT-6 Astra with web search alone. A general model maker is now selling directly into legal work.

## Figures

- 54%, correct answers by Astra for Law on 200 legal research questions, as reported by OpenAI
- 38.7%, correct answers by GPT-6 Astra with web search alone, on the same questions
- 230 million, URLs in the Legal Search Index
