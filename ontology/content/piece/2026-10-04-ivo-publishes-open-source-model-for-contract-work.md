## Summary

LawFuel reports that Ivo released Ivo Sage at its first user conference on 1 October 2026. Sage is an open-source model, built with River AI by further training DeepSeek V4 Flash on contract work. Ivo says it rose from 70% to 91% on the Legal Agent Benchmark Contracts test. Ivo also published a benchmark with micro1 that, as LawFuel reports it, found models accept counterparty changes correctly 82% of the time but counter-propose only 46% of the time. The results are Ivo's own, and Ivo sells contract software.

## Figures

- 70% to 91%, Sage's score on the Legal Agent Benchmark Contracts after reinforcement learning, per Ivo
- 82%, share of the time models accept counterparty changes correctly, per the Ivo and micro1 benchmark
- 46%, share of the time models counter-propose, per the same benchmark
- 23%, cases where models met escalation criteria, per the same benchmark
