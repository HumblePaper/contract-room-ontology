## Summary

Legaltech News reports that Legora has launched Legora Intake, a work management system for corporate legal departments. It takes requests from across a company, sorts them and routes the work according to the team's existing workflows and playbooks, using Legora's Skills feature. It starts in Slack, and the company plans to add email, Teams and Model Context Protocol connections. The page is behind a registration wall, so this summary covers only the opening of the article.
