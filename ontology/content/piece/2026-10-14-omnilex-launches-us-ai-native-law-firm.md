## Summary

Artificial Lawyer reports that Leon Johnson, a former Goodwin partner, has joined Omnilex as managing partner to open its US business. Omnilex is a Swiss-founded firm that uses its own AI as the first point of contact and has a lawyer review the output before it reaches the client. The US arm starts with venture financing, startup governance and commercial contracts. Johnson says about 75% of an M&A deal is project management that AI can handle today. He says the fixed fee will have to scale with the complexity of the matter.

## Figures

- US$4.5 million, Omnilex seed round raised in November 2025
- 100, legal teams Omnilex serves, roughly
- 20, people Omnilex employs, approximately
- 3, lawyers employed in the US arm so far
