## Summary

ComplexDiscovery says the Cooley and Google Cloud redaction agent is still in development, with no availability date, price or published error rate. It points out that Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 5.2 puts redaction duty on counsel, so a missed identifier is the filer's problem. The article names two failure modes: missing sensitive content, and applying redactions badly, such as boxes over text that is still underneath. It recommends four questions for vendors: which recall and precision were measured, on what data and by whom; how technical information is defined; how accepted redactions are applied and checked; and what audit record shows each lawyer's decision.
