## Summary

This is a vendor press release from Legora. It says Salesforce has deployed Legora across its Legal and Corporate Affairs organisation in North America, EMEA and APAC, next to its own Agentforce and Slack. Erica Swainson, Salesforce's VP of Legal Innovation, is quoted on freeing the team for work that needs its judgement. Legora says it serves over 100,000 legal professionals at more than 1,800 firms and in-house teams in 50-plus markets. The release ties the news to Dreamforce 2026.

## Figures

- 100 thousand plus, legal professionals using Legora, per Legora
- 1800 plus, firms and in-house teams using Legora, per Legora
