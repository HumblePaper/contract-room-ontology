## Summary

Dealroom News reports that Chamelio, an Israeli-founded legal technology company, raised a 26 million dollar Series A (the page does not state the currency) led by Entree Capital, five months after its seed round. Its software drafts, negotiates, routes approvals and tracks obligations, leaving complex decisions to lawyers. Chamelio says its annual recurring revenue has quadrupled since the seed and that hundreds of customers, including Wiz and monday.com, use it. The article ranks the round above the 92nd percentile of US legal Series A rounds historically, which is the top 8%. The company plans to expand product work and build its own legal AI models.
