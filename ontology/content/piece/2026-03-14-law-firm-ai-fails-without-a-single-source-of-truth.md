## Summary

This is a LinkedIn post by Ryan McKeen, whose headline could not be confirmed from the guest page. He says his firm found its files scattered across email, shared drives and different systems, so it first set up HubSpot as a single source of truth for marketing and sales data, and only then used Claude Code to automate workflows. He argues that every law firm should fix data and systems before buying new AI tools, because poor data gives poor results. The post concerns marketing and sales data, not contract work (date approximate).
