## Summary

LawSites reports that ROSS Intelligence, the legal research company that has shut down, plans to ask the US Supreme Court to review the Third Circuit's ruling for Thomson Reuters. Yar Chaikovsky, a White & Case partner acting for ROSS, said the ruling leaves uncertainty about how copyright law applies to training AI models. The dispute is over whether ROSS's use of Thomson Reuters material to train its system was infringement. Anyone who licenses data to an AI vendor, or buys AI tools trained on third-party content, has an interest in how the courts settle this.
