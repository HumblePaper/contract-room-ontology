## Summary

This is a LinkedIn post by Matt Buswell, who says he is a former in-house tech lawyer and now places in-house lawyers at tech companies. He argues that AI can review contracts, flag risks and draft clauses, but cannot stand up to sales or a customer's lawyer who pushes a company to accept a clause that undermines its business model, such as IP rights in a SaaS platform or termination for convenience from day one of a five-year subscription. His advice is to check the commercial logic, ask leadership about its appetite for risk, and answer with a firm, explained no or an approved alternative. The post gives no data (date approximate).
