## Summary

Bessemer Venture Partners, which has invested in Legora and so has an interest, publishes a case study of the company's growth. It says Legora reached US$100m in annual recurring revenue in 18 months and doubled to US$200m in under six months. It credits embedding legal engineers inside client teams, founder-led selling, and source citations that let lawyers check answers. It also says Legora moved from seat-based to consumption-based pricing as its product became more agentic. The figures are Bessemer's account of its portfolio company.

## Figures

- US$100m, Legora annual recurring revenue reached in 18 months, per Bessemer
- US$200m, annual recurring revenue less than six months later, per Bessemer
- 1800 plus, law firms and in-house teams using Legora in 50 plus markets, per Bessemer
