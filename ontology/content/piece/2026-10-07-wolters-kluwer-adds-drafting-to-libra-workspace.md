## Summary

ITdaily reports that Wolters Kluwer Legal & Regulatory has added Drafting to Libra, its legal AI workspace. Lawyers can draft, edit and review documents in a Word-style editor, or stay in the existing Word add-in. Firms can upload their own templates, which Libra applies to new documents, and changes stay visible for acceptance or suggestion. Wolters Kluwer's chief executive said combining research, review and drafting in one place lets lawyers automate more of the process. The article says later additions are planned but gives no dates.
