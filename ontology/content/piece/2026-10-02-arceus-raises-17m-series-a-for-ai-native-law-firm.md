## Summary

Dealroom News reports that Arceus, a law firm that combines staff attorneys with contract-review software, raised a 17 million dollar Series A (the page does not state the currency) led by Greycroft, with Craft Ventures and South Park Commons. Staff submit contracts from Slack, an agent pulls context from systems such as Salesforce and HubSpot, and a lawyer gives final approval. Arceus says it cuts review from days or weeks to hours and charges nothing if a review takes more than eight hours. The founder, Mac Liu, is not a lawyer.

## Figures

- 17 million dollars, Series A led by Greycroft, and the page does not state the currency
- 8 hours, review time beyond which Arceus says the review is free
