## Summary

Legaltech News reports that AI vendors, among them Anthropic, have begun moving from per-seat licences to pricing based on use in 2026, and that legal technology vendors are weighing the same step. Law firms are preparing ways to hold costs down, and the article names Troutman Pepper Locke as planning an internal mechanism to route work to different AI models. The page sits behind a registration wall, so this summary covers only the opening of the article. For a legal team it matters because AI cost would then grow with use, not stay fixed.
