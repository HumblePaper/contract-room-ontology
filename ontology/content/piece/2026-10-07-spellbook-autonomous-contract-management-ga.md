## Summary

This is Spellbook's own announcement that its Autonomous Contract Management is generally available. It collects agreements from email, Slack and forms, checks them against an organisation's standards, and routes them by rules and playbooks, and it can auto-approve contracts under US$50,000 that pass all playbook rules. A Deal Workspace holds each version, message and approval, and humans work alongside the AI through tracked changes in Word. Signed agreements go into a searchable repository that answers with citations. All claims are the vendor's, and the post gives no customer results.
