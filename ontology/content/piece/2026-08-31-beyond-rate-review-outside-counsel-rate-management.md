## Summary

This is a post on CLOC's site by Micky Colvin of Heidelberg Materials North America. She says reviewing every individual timekeeper becomes burdensome, and describes the Outside Counsel Rate Analysis agent her team built to compare rates against market data and past use. She proposes fixed fees for predictable work, matter budgets from past spend, standard rates by role, and multi-year agreements with set adjustment rules. Her point is to set expectations when a matter is planned, not argue over invoices afterward.
