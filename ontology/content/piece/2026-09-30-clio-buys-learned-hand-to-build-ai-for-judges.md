## Summary

Dealroom News reports that Clio has bought Learned Hand, whose software lets judges upload filings, pull out key facts and draft memos, orders and opinions with citations. The price was not disclosed, and the article gives Clio's last valuation as 5 billion dollars and says it bought vLex for 1 billion dollars last year (the page does not state the currency). It quotes Clio's founder, Jack Newton, saying state trial court judges are already buried under heavy caseloads and that he expects AI to add to the pressure. Separately, pro se federal cases rose to 16.8% in 2025 from an 11% average. Learned Hand had raised about 5 million dollars.
