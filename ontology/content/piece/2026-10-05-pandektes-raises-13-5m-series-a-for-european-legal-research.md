## Summary

The Next Web reports that Pandektes, a Copenhagen legal research startup, raised a EUR 13.5m Series A led by Munich's Alstin Capital, with PROfounders, Scale Capital and existing investors. The platform uses AI to gather and organise legal sources, and the founders say it serves more than 500 customers in Denmark, Germany and Switzerland. The money is for expansion into more European and North American markets. The article also reports the founders' view that LexisNexis, Westlaw, Beck and Karnov took one to two years to answer with AI search.

## Figures

- EUR 13.5m, Series A led by Alstin Capital
- 500, customers in Denmark, Germany and Switzerland, as reported by the founders
- sixfold, revenue growth over the past year, as reported
- EUR 2.9m, seed round raised in February 2025
