## Summary

This is a press release from the vendor, Legora. It says BMW Group is rolling out Legora across its Legal, IP and Compliance function for research, litigation management, contract review, regulatory monitoring and drafting from internal standards. Legora says the setup meets BMW's security and governance rules and works with tools such as Word. BMW's Group General Counsel, Dr Andreas Liepe, is quoted on the capacity to do more work in-house. The release also gives Legora's own customer counts, which it does not source.

## Figures

- 13 thousand, legal professionals in Germany using Legora, up from about 3,000 at the start of the year, per Legora
- over 150, law firms and in-house teams using Legora in the DACH region, per Legora
- 130 thousand, legal professionals at more than 2,000 organisations worldwide, per Legora
