## Summary

This is a LinkedIn post by Preston Clark, whose headline the guest page does not show, and it is more than three years old. He argues that contract lifecycle management (CLM) vendors charge per seat for business users, at over 1,000 dollars a year each, so a legal team cannot afford to let its whole sales team self-serve standard contracts such as an NDA. He says CLM workflows solve a legal operations problem and not a sales problem, so business licences belong in the platform fee. For a buyer, the post names seat pricing as a cost to test in a CLM quote (date approximate).
