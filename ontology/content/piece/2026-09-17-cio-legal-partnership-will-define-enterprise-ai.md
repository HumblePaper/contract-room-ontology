## Summary

Dan Clarke argues in CIO.com that chief information officers and legal teams must work together on AI governance, because each sees only part of the risk. Legal understands litigation and regulatory exposure, and IT understands deployment. He points to shadow AI (tools the organisation does not know about) and AI built into third-party products as key risks, and says existing law applies to AI-made decisions. He recommends a governance committee drawn from IT, legal, compliance, security and the business, a full inventory of AI use cases, and records that policies are enforced.

## Figures

- 88%, organisations regularly using AI in at least one business function, up from 78%, as cited from McKinsey's 2025 survey
- 56%, respondents who said managing generative AI litigation and legal risk had been a challenge, as cited from Norton Rose Fulbright's 2025 survey
