## Summary

The American Lawyer reports on associates who have chosen to use generative AI and describe it as helping their work. This runs against earlier Law.com research in which many midlevel lawyers named technology replacing humans as their greatest professional threat. The piece is part of the Pathways: Future of the Business of Law 2040 series. The page is behind a paywall, so this summary covers only the opening of the article.
