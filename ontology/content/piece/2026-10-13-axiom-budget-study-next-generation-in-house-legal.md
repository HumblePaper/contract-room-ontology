## Summary

This is Axiom's own sponsored piece on Above the Law. It promotes an on-demand webinar on Axiom's 2027 In-House Legal Budget series, which cites research from 510 legal and finance leaders in six countries. Speakers include executives from Ford, Google and Aon, and Richard Susskind. The topics are talent, AI adoption, outside counsel, legal operations and alternative service providers. The page gives no findings, so it points to a study without reporting what the study found.
