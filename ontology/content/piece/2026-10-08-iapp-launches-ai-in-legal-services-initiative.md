## Summary

This is a release from the IAPP, which sells the training and certification it describes. It announces an AI Specialist certification for lawyers, paralegals and legal operations staff, covering AI foundations, legal use cases, governance, legal ethics and professional responsibility. Organisations that join a launch supporters programme get early, discounted access. A new 19-member advisory board of people from law firms, legal departments, technology providers and universities will guide the work. The IAPP's chief executive said legal professionals need practical, trusted guidance.
