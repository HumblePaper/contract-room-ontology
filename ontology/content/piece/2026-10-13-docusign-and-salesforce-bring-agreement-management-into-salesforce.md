## Summary

This is a Docusign press release carried by PR Newswire. Docusign says its Intelligent Agreement Management platform now works natively in Salesforce, Agentforce and Slack. A seller can ask for an agreement in conversation, pull in live customer data and approved terms, and send it to legal in the same Slack thread. Docusign also says the contract data inside Salesforce can flag renewals and obligations buried in agreements. The release adds that Docusign was named a 2027 Salesforce Partner of the Year.
