## Summary

Artificial Lawyer reports that Z.ai, the company behind the GLM models, has released GLM-Legal, built on its GLM-5.3-Flash model with extra training on legal tasks such as contract review and regulatory compliance. It is offered as a stand-alone legal model, not an add-in. Z.ai says it tested the model against benchmarks that include Harvey's. The article gives no price and notes that Chinese legal tech surged a decade ago, then faded, and is growing again. For deal makers it is a sign that legal models are arriving from outside the Western vendors.
