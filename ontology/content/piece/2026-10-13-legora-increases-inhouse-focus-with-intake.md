## Summary

Artificial Lawyer reports that Legora has launched Intake, a front door for in-house legal requests. Requests arrive through channels such as Slack, where an agent asks follow-up questions, assigns an owner and a due date, and prepares a first draft from the team's stored playbooks and precedents. For routine pre-approved requests, such as standard NDAs, the agent can complete the task with no lawyer involved. Legora says in-house teams are its fastest-growing UK client group. Intake also logs tasks, so legal leaders can see what the business asks of them.
