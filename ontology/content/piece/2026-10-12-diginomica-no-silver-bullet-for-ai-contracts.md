## Summary

diginomica's Derek du Preez argues that enterprise buyers of AI services should not expect the contract to carry the risk, because most protection has to be built inside the buying company. He says buyers should separate use of their data for inference from use for training, and watch for vague clauses that let a vendor reuse data. With no precedent on who is liable when an AI system causes harm, the article reports expert advice to negotiate a workable liability cap and to keep contracts to a year or less. It also lists internal steps such as logging data flows and requiring human approval of agent actions.
