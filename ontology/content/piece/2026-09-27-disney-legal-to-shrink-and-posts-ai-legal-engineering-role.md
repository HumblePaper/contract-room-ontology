## Summary

IBTimes UK reports that on 18 September Horacio Gutierrez, Disney's chief legal and global affairs officer, told staff the division will be much smaller than today, with no numbers or dates given. The review will weigh automation, self-service, alternative providers and outsourcing. Six days later Disney posted a Director role for AI enablement and legal engineering, covering contract review automation and legal research tools. The article notes the timing but says Disney has not said AI will replace specific legal jobs. It puts the division at fewer than 1,000 people.
