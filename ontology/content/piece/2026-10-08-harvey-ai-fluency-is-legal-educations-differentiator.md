## Summary

This is a vendor post on Harvey's own blog, and Harvey sells to the law schools it discusses. Keith Enright argues that law schools should build AI training into their courses, because employers expect graduates who can use it. He says educators' worry about weaker analytical skills and employers' worry about training new hires in tools are compatible, since judgment and AI fluency reinforce each other. He names the University of Chicago, Pepperdine and DePaul as schools with different approaches. Harvey says it works with more than 100 law schools worldwide.
