## Summary

This is a vendor post on Thomson Reuters' own legal blog, about its own legal department using its own CoCounsel product. The company's chief legal officer, Norie Campbell, and deputy general counsel, Bertil Majer, led a change that took contracting from 33 order forms to 3. The team used CoCounsel to study past negotiations for patterns in customer concessions and friction. It also produced AI-assisted playbooks so the deal desk can handle routine matters without a lawyer. One team member says work that took a day now takes about an hour.
