## Summary

Vals AI published this benchmark with Legaltech Hub in February 2025, about twenty months before this summary was written, and it tested four legal AI tools against a group of lawyers on seven tasks. The best tool beat the lawyers on answering questions about a document, 94.8% against 70.1%, but trailed them on redlining, 65.0% against 79.7%. The report is one of the few published measurements of AI against lawyers on contract work. It advises using the tools only for certain redlining tasks. Vendors chose which tasks to enter, and Vals AI has a customer relationship with one or more of them.
