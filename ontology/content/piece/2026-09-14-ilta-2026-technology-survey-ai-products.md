## Summary

LawSites reports the International Legal Technology Association's 2026 technology survey, which covers more than 500 member firms in 12 countries. The survey found that 94% of firms use or are exploring generative AI, up from 80%. Microsoft 365 Copilot led at 76% of firms, while Thomson Reuters CoCounsel, Harvey and Anthropic's Claude each reached 44% of firms and Legora 16%. Among firms of 700 or more lawyers, Copilot stood at 88% and Harvey at 84%. Only Copilot (52%) and Westlaw Advantage (50%) were fully deployed at half or more of firms.
