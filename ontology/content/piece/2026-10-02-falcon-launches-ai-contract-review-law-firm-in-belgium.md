## Summary

Dealroom News reports that Falcon, a new Belgian legal services firm, launched with EUR 1.5m. It runs as a managed service: AI reviews each contract against the client's own playbook, and a lawyer signs off. Clients pay a fixed monthly fee and get same-day turnaround. It starts with NDAs, data processing agreements and master services agreements for small and mid-sized businesses, and Loop Earplugs is an early paying client. The article quotes Legal Technology for a European commercial legal services market of about EUR 49bn a year.

- EUR 1.5m, funding at launch
