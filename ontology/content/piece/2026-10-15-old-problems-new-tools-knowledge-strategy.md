## Summary

This is a post in Artificial Lawyer by Patrick DiDomenico, founder and CEO of LegalTech Connect. He argues that the tools are better than they have ever been, yet the problems are the same as ever. Lawyers still draft, negotiate and litigate as before, and the hard part remains getting busy lawyers to record and share what they know. He adds that AI brings new problems of its own, namely hallucinations and a bottleneck of reviewers checking AI output. His advice is to back knowledge work with sponsorship, incentives and documented process, and to train existing staff instead of hunting for one person with every skill.
