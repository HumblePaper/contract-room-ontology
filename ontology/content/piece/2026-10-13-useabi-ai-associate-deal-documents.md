## Summary

LawSites reports that Abdulrasheed Ijaodola, a former M&A lawyer at Greenberg Traurig, has launched useABI, an AI associate for deals, with co-founder Adam Usman. It produces purchase agreement markups, issues lists, diligence reports and checklists in about 30 minutes, without being prompted for each. It ties diligence findings to the issues list and the markup, and shapes its output to a firm's past deals. An unnamed Am Law 100 firm is its first major deployment, in a pilot. The report relies on the company's own account, and the notable claim is that one system links diligence to the markup.

## Figures

- 30 minutes, the time the company says it takes to produce a deliverable
- 45, deals Ijaodola closed as an M&A lawyer, worth more than US$15 billion in total
- 71, categories of deal information in the Insights feature
