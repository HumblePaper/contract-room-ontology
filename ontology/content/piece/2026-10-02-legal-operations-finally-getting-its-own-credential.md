## Summary

This is a post on CLOC's site by board member Carl Morrison about the Legal Operations Certified Professional credential. It rests on CLOC's Core 12 competencies and will be examined with real-world scenarios, with ethics as a core skill and the AI content updated as the exam is revised. An independent certification board will govern it, and the first exam is set for May 2027 at CLOC's Global Institute in Las Vegas. Morrison says 53% of survey respondents called credentialing high value. CLOC runs the credential, so the post is the sponsor's own case for it.
