## Summary

LawSites reports that Newcode, a legal AI company based in Oslo, closed a Series A led by OnDean Forward, bringing its 2026 funding to US$20 million, which by the article's arithmetic makes the round about US$13.5 million. Newcode sells configurable AI infrastructure rather than a finished assistant, so a firm controls its own AI, with granular permission controls and a claimed 700+ MCP connections. It serves more than 60 firms and government bodies, including The Bar of Ireland, and earlier reporting identified DLA Piper as a customer. The company says its annual recurring revenue tripled in twelve months, and the money funds a U.S. push.

- US$20 million, Newcode's total funding in 2026
