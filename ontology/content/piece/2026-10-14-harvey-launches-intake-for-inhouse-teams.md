## Summary

Artificial Lawyer reports that Harvey has launched Intake, a front door for in-house legal teams built with Carvana, GE Aerospace and Dentsu. Intake collects work requests from the tools a company already uses, sends them to an agent or a lawyer, and runs a first contract review against the team's playbook. A Carvana executive is quoted saying it can apply the playbook to a contract and have an initial review ready for sign-off. Legora launched a similar product the same week, and the article calls the pair the opening salvo in agentic handling of contract work for corporate legal teams.

## Figures

- 1600, in-house legal teams Harvey says it serves, including 5 of the Fortune 10
