## Summary

This is a vendor post on Harvey's own blog. Harvey says it is adding Legalscape's Japanese legal research, covering statutes, case law, administrative guidelines and commentary, to its platform. Named uses include Japanese M&A, cross-border disputes with Japanese parties and regulatory checks on Japanese operations. The feature will arrive in the coming months, with early access through Harvey account teams. Harvey describes Legalscape as serving more than 3,000 clients and says its own network holds over 1,000 legal data sources.

## Figures

- 1000 plus, legal data sources in Harvey's network, per Harvey
- 3000 plus, Legalscape clients, per Harvey
