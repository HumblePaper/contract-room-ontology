## Summary

Artificial Lawyer reports that Legora has built an ecosystem of more than 30 integrations, so lawyers can reach their other systems without leaving its platform. The list covers document management (iManage, NetDocuments), data rooms (Datasite, Intralinks), ethical walls, billing, and contract management tools including DocuSign, Ironclad, Sirion, Agiloft and Conga. Legora's chief product officer, Bryan Tsao, is quoted saying every trip to another system eats into the time AI saves. For a legal team choosing a contract tool, the article shows that the fight among vendors is now over which one becomes the place where the work happens.
