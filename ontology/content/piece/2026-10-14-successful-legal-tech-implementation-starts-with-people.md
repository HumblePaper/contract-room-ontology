## Summary

This is Associate News content provided by Legal Futures Associate Osprey Approach, which reports an episode of its podcast. The episode features the advice of David Langdon, a legal technology consultant and author of Beyond the Features. He says firms should speak to staff in every department before approaching vendors, then judge systems on integration, security, usability and support rather than long feature lists. He recommends involving the lawyers, secretaries and finance staff who run the workflows, resisting heavy customisation, and planning adoption beyond go-live with change champions and staged training. The advice is general and not tied to any one product.
