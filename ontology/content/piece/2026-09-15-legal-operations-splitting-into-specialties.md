## Summary

This is a post on CLOC's site by Stacy Lettie of Johnson & Johnson, who argues that legal operations is no longer one job. She names five specialties: outside counsel and financial management, contract and document management, technology, strategy and innovation, and law firms. In contract and document management, she says the work has moved from basic knowledge management to document intelligence, where AI tools pull data out of contracts without manual entry. Her advice to hirers is to decide first what problem they are solving, and to hire for that specialty.
