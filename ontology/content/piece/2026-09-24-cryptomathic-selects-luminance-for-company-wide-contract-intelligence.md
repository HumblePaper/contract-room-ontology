## Summary

This is a Luminance press release republished by LawFuel. It says that Cryptomathic, a Danish IT security company, has adopted Luminance's contract platform so that sales, finance, sales operations and IT can ask questions of its contracts in plain language. Its lean legal team had been fielding routine contract queries from across the business. The company's general counsel, Peter Istrup, is quoted saying staff can find answers themselves without going through Legal. Cryptomathic chose Luminance partly for quick set-up on existing contracts and for pricing by document volume rather than per user, and the account is Luminance's own.
