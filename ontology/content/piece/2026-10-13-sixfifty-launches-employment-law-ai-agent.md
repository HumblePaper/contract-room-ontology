## Summary

Legaltech News reports that SixFifty has launched an AI agent that helps legal, HR and compliance teams answer employment law questions and draft documents, contracts included. SixFifty began inside Wilson Sonsini and was sold to the payroll provider Paychex in 2025. SixFifty's agent targets companies with small or no in-house legal teams and tailors its output to each jurisdiction.
