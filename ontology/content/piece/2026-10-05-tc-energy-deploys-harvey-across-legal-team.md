## Summary

This is a customer announcement on the vendor's own blog, from Harvey. It says TC Energy has deployed Harvey across its legal team, which works across Canada, the United States and Mexico. During the pilot TC Energy set a Harvey-first rule, asking lawyers to start their work in the tool. Harvey lists litigation, regulatory work, M&A, commercial contracting and compliance as the areas of wider use. The post gives no usage numbers.
