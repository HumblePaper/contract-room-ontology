## Summary

LawSites rounds up product news from the ILTACON legal technology conference. For contract work, the main item is Avvoka's Curate, which reads a firm's past deal documents, finds the strongest wording for each clause and lets lawyers choose preferred positions and fallbacks as the base for future drafts. Avvoka built it with Am Law 100 and international firms in private beta. The roundup also covers iManage plans for Google Cloud's Gemini Enterprise, NetDocuments adding Gemini to its MCP ecosystem, and Thomson Reuters releasing CoCounsel Legal to general availability. All of it is vendor-announced.
