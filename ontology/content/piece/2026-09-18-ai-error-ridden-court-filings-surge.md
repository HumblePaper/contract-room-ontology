## Summary

Complete AI Training, in a summary of Reuters reporting, says AI-generated fabrications have appeared in at least 1,395 US court filings, three years after the first sanctions. It cites recent cases in New Mexico and Oklahoma and a State Farm lawyer fined over wrong citations, and it is a secondary account, not the Reuters text. Judge P. Kevin Castel, who issued the first sanctions in 2023, is quoted saying the need for sanctions that deter more grows as the problem continues. UCLA's Mark McKenna blames competitive pressure, tight deadlines and AI marketing that plays down risk.
